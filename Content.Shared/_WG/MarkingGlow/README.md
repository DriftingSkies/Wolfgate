# MarkingGlow

Players can make a character's markings, eyes and hair glow: drawn unshaded, so they stay bright in the dark. In the
character creator each applied marking on the Markings tab has a Glow switch (`WolfgateMarkingPicker`). The Hair card
has "Glowing hair" and "Glowing facial hair", and the Eyes card has "Glowing eyes"
(`HumanoidProfileEditor.MarkingGlow.cs`). Markings whose prototype already sets a shader get no switch.

The profile stores the choices as `MarkingGlow`, a sorted list of marking IDs plus the keys `@eyes`, `@hair` and
`@facial_hair` (`MarkingGlowRules`). It is saved `;`-separated in the `marking_glow` profile column. Hair glows by its key
whatever style is picked. Validation drops IDs of markings the character no longer wears.

On profile load the list is copied to `HumanoidAppearanceComponent.MarkingGlow`, which is networked. The client's
`HumanoidAppearanceSystem` then sets the `unshaded` shader on the layers of glowing markings and on the eye layer.
Erasing the eyes with a custom marking keeps their glow, because the erase mask picks its unshaded variant from the
eye layer's shader.

`wg.marking_glow.enabled` (replicated, default on) turns the feature off: bodies are drawn lit, the creator hides the
switches, and saved choices are kept.
