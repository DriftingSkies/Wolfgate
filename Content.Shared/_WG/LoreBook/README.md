# LoreBook

A book-themed window that holds the setting's lore, split out of the guidebook. Any guide entry with `lore: true`, and
every child under it, belongs to the lore book: the guidebook leaves it out, and any request for it (a job's help, a
book's `GuideHelp`, a `textlink`) opens the lore book at that page instead. Pages are numbered in reading order, with a
searchable contents page on the left.

Players open it from the book button on the top bar, the game menu or the lobby. Guide pages link into it with
`[lorelink="text" link="EntryId"]`, a gold link with a book icon. Entities with a lore page get a Lore button on
examine: list them in a `wgLoreLink` prototype (children of a listed prototype inherit it), or give them a
`GuideHelp` that points at a lore page.

Some pages unsettle the book. A guide entry's `unease` (0 to 1) builds up as it is read in an open book
(`WGLoreBookWindow.Unease.cs`): letters on every page start to slip, then the book writes lines of its own between
paragraphs (`wg-lore-book-whisper-*`), and deep in, its title, chapter trail and page numbers slip too. Closing the
book and opening it again clears it. A page stays as it was drawn until it is turned again; now and then a word is written in the DS13 glyph font
(`WGLoreGlyphs`), and quiet whispers play under the book (`WGLoreBookWhispers`), more often and louder as it corrupts.
Pages that carry unease (the Monolith, the Unmooring, the Lattice, the Choir) sit together in the Forbidden chapter at the back. Pages turn with one of two page-flip sounds (`WGLoreBookPageTurn`).

Raising `WGLoreBookUIController.Edition` opens the book once for every player, at its front, on their next lobby
or round; `wg.lorebook.seen_edition` (client, saved) remembers who has seen it.

Entry points: `WGLoreBookUIController` (open, toggle, link redirects), `WGLoreBookWindow`, `WGLoreBookSystem` (which
entries and entities are lore, the examine verb), `WGLoreLinkTag`, `GuideEntry.Lore` and `WGLoreLinkPrototype`.
Textures come from `Tools/_WG/LoreBook/make_textures.py`. Lore pages live in `Resources/ServerInfo/_WG/LoreBook` and
in the upstream `_Mono` lore, factions and star system pages.
