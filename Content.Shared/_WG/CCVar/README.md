# CCVar

Wolfgate's CVar definitions, kept out of upstream `CCVars`. `WolfgateCVars` holds the general ones (`wg.ui_style`,
`wg.anatomy_enabled`, `wg.anatomy_notice_seen`, `wg.ui_window_popout`, `wg.spawn_favourites`);
`CollisionWarningCVars`, `FeedbackCVars`, `HeatHazeCVars`, `InternetSoundCVars`, `SoundtrackMuteCVars`, `NpcCrewCVars`,
`ShipAccessCVars` and `ShockwaveCVars` tune the collision warning, player bug reports and suggestions, heat haze,
internet sounds, soundtrack mute, NPC crew docking, ship access locking and explosion shockwaves;
`PlanetCVars` holds the planet network, terrain atmosphere and terrain loading and unloading settings,
`PlanetCommsCVars` the planetary comms gate (`wg.planet_comms`), `CavernCVars` the cavern switch and `BenchCVars` the
Bench's ticket range, debt deadline, debt cap, garnish share and outlaw bounty.
