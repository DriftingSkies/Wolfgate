# Ambience

Wolfgate replacements for the Crescent biome and station music, swapped in a pool at a time. Tracks live in `Resources/Audio/_WG/Ambience` and are wired in through marked edits to the sound collections in `Resources/Prototypes/_Crescent/Music/ambient_music.yml`. Replaced so far: the Inner, Middle and Outer rings, the four Outer Cloud rings, and the INSO-357k and LINEAR-21 beacon clusters. The fallback for uncharted space (`BiomeDefault`) plays the Outer Cloud pool. Crocus, hyperspace, expeditions, stations and combat still use the Crescent tracks.

`ContentAudioSystem.Ambience.cs` fixes how ambient music plays: the next track starts when the current one ends (paused tracks wait), each pool shuffles so every track plays before a repeat, and a grid or biome change must hold for 3 s before the music follows it, with a 4 s crossfade.

`ContentAudioSystem.LatticeMusic.cs` plays a Lattice band's `music` (set per band in `Resources/Prototypes/_WG/Lattice/lattice.yml`) once on entering it, then fades out and holds off other ambient music until the player has been out of the band for 3 s (the band is worked out on the client from the field); the piece re-arms once they are back inside its edge. Feedback plays The Sentients and the Coda The Abyss (`Resources/Prototypes/_WG/Ambience/lattice_music.yml`).
