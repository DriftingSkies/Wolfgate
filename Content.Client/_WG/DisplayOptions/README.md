# DisplayOptions

Options controls for display settings that already existed as cvars but had no control in Game Options. Graphics:
a frame rate cap (`display.max_fps`) under Display, "Show the space background" (`parallax.enabled`), and the
light bloom toggle, bloom strength and film grain from the SCP layer (`scp.light_bloom_enable`,
`scp.light_bloom_strength`, `shader.grain_toggle_overlay`) under Quality. Misc: "Outline what's under the cursor"
(`outline.enabled`) under Cursor.

`outline.enabled` and `parallax.enabled` weren't saved between launches, so marked edits add `CVar.ARCHIVE` to them. The
`toggleoutline` command now persists too.

Light bloom is Wolfgate's own, under the SCP layer's cvars, which players already have saved. The SCP version drew a
hazy sprite over every lamp and washed out the screen around it, so a marked line keeps its overlays off.
`WGBloomOverlay` is a screen-space bloom instead: per viewport it box-filters the lit screen to half size, keeping only
what is near full brightness (largest channel over 0.85, with a soft knee), blurs that with a separable Gaussian across
and then down, and screen-blends it back over the world, below the other world-space overlays. Lamps, glowing sprites
and the brightest lit spots get a soft halo in their own colour; ordinary lit floors don't. The strength slider scales
the glow, and its default of 0.25 is subtle. It costs one screen copy and three half size passes per viewport. The
threshold, spread and gain are constants at the top of the shaders and the overlay.

Entry points: `GraphicsTab.WgAddDisplayOptions`, called from the `GraphicsTab` constructor, and a marked line in
`MiscTab` for the outline checkbox. `WGBloomSystem` adds `WGBloomOverlay` while `scp.light_bloom_enable` is on.
