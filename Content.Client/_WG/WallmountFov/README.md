# WallmountFov

Wall lights no longer show through the walls they hang on. Since Monolith's wallening change (`fd70ab3d0da`,
"WALLENING FIX SHIT (#4488)") a wall light stands on the floor tile but draws its sprite one tile over, on the wall
itself (`offset: 0, 1`). Hard FOV deliberately shows wall tiles from either side, so a lamp on a wall's far face stayed
on screen, and its unshaded glow and bloom lit up rooms the viewer couldn't see.

`WGWallBackfaceSystem` hides those sprites, client side, while the current eye is past the wall face: half a tile out
from the mount, in the direction of the sprite's offset turned by the mount's world rotation. It only acts while
Clyde would apply hard FOV to that eye, so ghosts, admin observers and FOV-less cameras still see every lamp. The
bloom overlay skips the lamps it hides. Nothing changes for other viewers or on the server.

Entry points: the `WGWallBackfaceVisuals` marker on the wall light bases (`AlwaysPoweredWallLight`,
`AlwaysPoweredStrobeLight`), `WGWallBackfaceSystem.IsBehindMount` for the test, and `WGWallBackfaceSystem.IsHidden`
for the bloom overlay. The wall's direction comes from the live sprite offset, so a child that draws on its own tile
(the free-standing lamps and signs in `DecorImport`) is never hidden; the `Visuals` suffix keeps the server from
rejecting the client-only component.
