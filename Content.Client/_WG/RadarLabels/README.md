# RadarLabels

Keeps the mass scanner's tags readable at any zoom. Every tag the scope draws (ship and station IFF labels, beacons
and worlds, encounter names, dock names, waypoints, planet signals and map sites, Lattice phantoms and the admin NPC
crew tags) goes to one layout pass instead of being drawn on the spot. A tag sits by its contact while it has room.
Tags that would overlap share one plain stacked list, its rows in the order of their contacts top to bottom, and the
list splits back into separate tags as zooming in makes room. A stack shows only each tag's first line; past ten
rows it keeps the highest priorities (hovered contact, player ships, encounters and waypoints) and sums up the rest.

The scope is quiet by default. Only tags marked `always` show their name anywhere on it: ships and encounter
ships within 750 m, the hovered contact, waypoints, beacons, your expedition, cities and depots, Lattice phantoms and
admin crew tags. Every other contact is a blip, with a `×N` count where several crowd together. Round the cursor a faint lens
(32 px) shows every tag, stacking as above, and the tag nearest the cursor alone shows its detail lines (company,
coordinates, track ID, a world's signals). Tags fade in and out over 0.15 s. Only contacts on the scope get a tag:
one pinned to the rim keeps its blip. Every radar marks docks as pink squares with a small minimum size on screen;
zoomed far out, where a dock would be a speck, they hide, and dock names show only once the radar is zoomed in
(`ShuttleNavControl.Docks.cs`). Only the
admin radar has a docks toggle. The
`wg.radar_smart_tags` option (Options, Wolfgate) turns all of this off, so every tag sits whole by its contact as
before.

Two more options sit beside it. `wg.radar_icons_only` ("Radar shows only icons") leaves just the icons: no names,
not even the always ones, no `×N` counts and no lens circle; hovering a contact shows its tag alone, details and all.
`wg.radar_hover_reveal` (on by default) turns the cursor's part off: no lens, and a pointed-at contact names itself
only if it would anyway. Icons only with hover reveal off is a bare scope; the all-names toggle still names everything.

The radar mode buttons carry an all-names toggle (`wg.radar_all_names`): every contact on the scope shows its name, and
the lens only adds details. Contacts on the scope are drawn as what they are (`ShuttleNavControl.RadarIcons.cs`): a ship
as a chevron pointing its heading, a station as a square with a dot, an asteroid as a rough hexagon, a wreck as a cross.
Unknown contacts and those pinned to the rim keep the old triangle. Encounter markers and NPC crew tags follow the IFF
toggle like any other contact.

Entry points: `WGRadarTagLayout` (the grouping, pure geometry) and `ShuttleNavControl.RadarLabels.cs`, whose
`WgBeginTag`, `WgTagLine`, `WgTagAbove` and `WgTagHeadExtra` a label source calls in place of `DrawString`.
`WgDrawTags` draws them all at the end of the scope's `Draw`.
