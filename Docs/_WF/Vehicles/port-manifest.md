# Vehicles port manifest

Where the Vehicles module's files come from and under which licence. The files carry no licence headers; this manifest
is the record.

## Sources

- RMC-14 (`rmc`), commit `e4627aee9e5bbd2d3841ddaa6c25593529a0638a` of 2026-10-09, engine v264. MIT. The framework:
  the base layer, grid mover, hardpoints, interiors, turrets, weapons, UI, and the APC, humvee, tank and van content.
- Colonial Marines Universe (`cmu`), commit `b3cac1a18a631ad5669e0255eaa4a0a4b3add0f6` of 2026-10-10, engine v290.
  AGPL-3.0 (all the CMU vehicle work in scope dates from September and October 2026, after the licence change). The
  extra vehicles, hardpoints and tank guns, six interior maps, emergency lights and the Bison cargo carrier. Also the
  car-style steering of the grid mover: the steered drive step (`UpdateSteeredMovement`) in
  `GridVehicleMoveSystem.Movement.cs`, `GetEffectiveSteering` and `CanSteer` in `GridVehicleMotionSimulator.cs`, the
  steering fields of `GridVehicleMoverComponent` and the turn rates on the vehicle prototypes. Those three files are
  otherwise RMC-14's, so they carry both licences. The same goes for `IsFrontImpact` in the simulator, the plow fields
  of the mover component, and the two tank flamers with their charges, jets and fuel tanks in `Hardpoints/tank_weapons.yml`,
  `Weapons/projectiles.yml` and `Weapons/ammo.yml`.
- RobustToolbox 264.0.2 (`engine`, RMC-14's fork), commit `ea09bdafbf0f55919b237960c4d7ed534daed9f8`. MIT. Only the
  per-fixture-pair loop of `IsHardCollidable`.

Wolfgate as a whole is AGPL-3.0, which admits both. Sprites keep the licence and copyright in their RSI `meta.json`;
audio and tile attributions are in `Resources/Audio/_WF/Vehicles/attributions.yml` and
`Resources/Textures/_WF/Vehicles/Tiles/attributions.yml`. The prototypes, locale and maps derived from RMC-14 are MIT, from CMU AGPL-3.0, like the C#.
