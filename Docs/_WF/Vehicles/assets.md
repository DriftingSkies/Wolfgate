# Vehicles assets

Textures, tile textures and audio of the Vehicles port, where each came from and under which licence. Sprites keep
the licence and copyright of their RSI `meta.json`; tile textures and audio carry an `attributions.yml` next to them.

The copy is `python Tools/_WF/Vehicles/copy_assets.py`, driven by `Tools/_WF/Vehicles/asset_files.yml`. It copies an
RSI as its `meta.json` plus the PNGs of its states, never changes a licence or copyright string, and writes the
attribution files. `--paths` prints `old new` for every sprite and sound path, which is what a prototype port needs to
rewrite (`_RMC14/Structures/Vehicles/apc.rsi` becomes `_WF/Vehicles/apc.rsi`).

## Layout

- `Resources/Textures/_WF/Vehicles`: RMC's `Structures/Vehicles` tree as it was (`*.rsi`, `hardpoints/`, `Interiors/`,
  `ammo/`), plus `Actions`, `Markers`, `Objects`, `Furniture`, `Wallmounts`, `Projectiles`, `Tiles` and, for CMU, `CMU/`
  (`fv150`, `hybrisa`, `marshalpaddywagon.rsi`, `cargo_carrier.rsi`).
- `Resources/Audio/_WF/Vehicles`: RMC's `Audio/_RMC14/Vehicle` (engine, horn and overdrive sounds at the top, `weapons/`),
  plus `effects/` (crash and hit sounds) and `guns/` (the sounds the APC port gun and the tank launcher use).
- Tile prototypes are in `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Vehicles/Tiles`, names in `Resources/Locale/en-US/_WF/Vehicles/tiles.ftl`.

## Origin that is not confirmed

RMC-14 ships these without an attribution file, so the licence below is a best guess, not a statement by the author.

- `Resources/Audio/_WF/Vehicles/*.ogg` and `weapons/*.ogg` (23 files). Added in RMC-14 PR #8791 by Vixting. The names
  look like cmss13's, and `smokelauncher_fire.ogg` from the same PR is credited to cmss13 in RMC (a re-encode of the
  same 1.91 s sound), so they are taken to be cmss13 sounds under CC-BY-SA-3.0. Colonial Marines Universe lists the same
  files as MIT, RMC-14 contributors, citing the same PR. The ogg files carry no tags.
- `Resources/Textures/_WF/Vehicles/Tiles/vehicle_interior` (114 PNGs). Added in the same PR with no source. Taken to be
  derived from cmss13 turf sprites under CC-BY-SA-3.0. The PNGs carry no tags.
- `Resources/Textures/_WF/Vehicles/Tiles/void.png`. Added in RMC-14 PR #8391 (Bosenmori Basho Content). A 32x32 filler
  tile; no source given.

Every other file has a licence from its RSI `meta.json` or from RMC's own `attributions.yml`.

## Left behind

- RSIs: `digger`, `powerloader`, `powerloader_clamp`, `vehicles`, `vehicles2`, `vehicles3` (props and the power loader),
  `hardpoints/truck.rsi`, `Interiors/apc_pmc.rsi`, `Interiors/clf_van.rsi`, `Interiors/general_wy.rsi` (no prototype in
  either repo uses these four), the supply console and lift art, the flamer projectile, `map_blips_large.rsi` (tactical
  map), the vendor `guns.rsi`, and `Blackfoot`, dropship and fighter art.
- Audio: `flamethrower.ogg` (flamers are deferred), the uncredited duplicate of `smokelauncher_fire.ogg`, CMU's weapon
  sounds (no attribution anywhere in CMU) and CMU's tank loops (`tank_engine_loop.wav` is under a Sonniss bundle
  licence, the `tank_t34` files have no attribution; `tank_boost_rev.wav` is CC0 but unused).
- Already in Wolfgate and not copied: `Audio/Effects/Vehicle/carhorn.ogg` and `vehicleengineidle.ogg`,
  `Audio/Weapons/Guns/Gunshots/flaregun.ogg`, `grenade_launcher.ogg` and `minigun.ogg`, `Audio/Weapons/Guns/Hits/bullet_hit.ogg`,
  `Audio/Effects/beep1.ogg` and `Objects/Weapons/Guns/Ammunition/Casings/large_casing.rsi`.

## Changes to a source RSI

- `tank.rsi` gains `aev_base`, `mountedflag_0`, `mountedflag_1` and `re700cupola_0` from CMU's copy, and
  `hardpoints/tank.rsi` gains `mounted_flag` and `re700_cupola`. CMU's own `tank_base` and `towlauncher_0` differ from
  RMC's and are not taken. Licence is unchanged; the copyright of `tank.rsi` is CMU's, which is RMC's plus a sentence
  crediting the `aev_base` art to CMSS13-PvE. CMU's copyright names no author for the other five merged states
  (`mountedflag_0`, `mountedflag_1`, `re700cupola_0`, `mounted_flag`, `re700_cupola`), and `hardpoints/tank.rsi` has only the
  plain CMSS13 sentence, so they are uncredited here too; they stand on the RSI's CC-BY-SA-3.0 as CMU ships it. The table
  says so on both rows (`uncredited` in `asset_files.yml`); the author is for CMU to say.
- `Markers/landmarks.rsi` keeps `invisible_wall` and `vehicle_blocker` (of 188 states), and `Furniture/chairs.rsi` keeps
  `comfychair` (of 25).
