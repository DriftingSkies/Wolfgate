# PdvArtificer

The PDV Artificer, the Vanguard's engineer and the counterpart of the TSFMC engineer. It needs 6 hours overall, as
the TSFMC engineer does, ranks with the Rakhshan in crew deployment and uses the Rakhshan chat ranks. It spawns with
an engineering encryption key, a recharging RCD, two foam grenades and a utility pouch, and has its own loadout groups
for mesons or a diagnostic HUD and an engineering toolbelt. Its specialist kit is the Artificer kit
(`SpecialistKits`).

Helios Fortress has 4 slots and two `WFSpawnPointPdvArtificer` markers beside the Jarrah's in the spawn hall. The
ID card, PDA and job icon reuse the Rakhshan art.
