# Cooking

The cooking rework: hands-on cooking after Roguetown's hearths in the owner's Paragon Moon project (code rewritten for
SS14; art CC BY-SA 3.0), with ideas from Meridian. It replaces the game's microwave-style cooking: the station
microwave becomes an oven-only hearth and the electric range a hearth with a hob and an oven (marked edits). Nothing
becomes a meal from raw ingredients in one step.

- **Hearth** (`WGHearthComponent`): a hob slot for one pan or pot and an oven. The hearth stove burns wood like a
  bonfire (`WGBonfireFuel`: fed planks or logs, lit with a flame); the converted microwave and range work while powered,
  and the camp stove needs neither. Use cookware
  on it to set it on the hob; use food on it to fry it, stew it or bake it. An empty hand takes food out of the pan,
  then the oven, then the cookware off.
- **Frying and baking** (`wgCookRecipe`): food takes heat each second and becomes its result after the recipe's
  seconds; left on, it smokes, then burns to a mess. Food with no such recipe still cooks if heat alone makes it
  something: a kitchen recipe of that one item and no reagents (dough into bread, cake batter into cake), or its
  construction graph's heating step (raw meat, cutlets and bacon into cooked ones, coffee beans roasting on).
- **Stews** (`wgStewRecipe`): a pot of water boils on a lit hob; an ingredient added to boiling water becomes a portion
  of soup, and ingredients stewing together that make a stew of several (`combine`) become it instead. Soup shows in
  pots and bowls in its colour; ladle it out with a bowl.
- **Prep board** (`wgPrepRecipe`, `wgChopRecipe`, and every kitchen recipe): lay ingredients on it, chop them with a
  knife, then *Put together* (alt-click). Our raw dishes and the game's assembler recipes come out ready to cook or
  eat; every game oven or microwave recipe comes out as an unbaked dish (`WGRawKitchenDish`) that still has to be cooked
  in a hearth's oven or pan. A secret recipe (`secretRecipe`) is offered only by a board within reach of a hearth that
  provides it (`FoodRecipeProvider`, such as the Donk Co. microwave and range), and only such a hearth takes and cooks
  it.
- **One recipe book**: the game's `microwaveMealRecipe`s cook by hand as above, and each of our raw dishes is also a
  `microwaveMealRecipe`.
- **Brewing** (`wgBrewRecipe`, `brewing.yml`, `WGCookingSystem.Brewing`): only a kettle (`WGPot` with `kettle: true`)
  brews, and it makes no soup. Items steep like a stew's ingredients; tea powders and coffee grounds steep a little
  each second. Mono's and Frontier's powder-and-hot-water reactions are commented out (marked edits), so pouches brew
  only in a kettle, and the barista vendor sells our kettle, teapot, cups and a camp stove.
- **Cup buff** (`WGMealSystem.Cup`, `WGWarmCupComponent`): a kettle drink's `WGMealBuff` with `cup: true` fills a
  second slot beside the meal's; a trait both give works once, at the stronger. Each sip gives comfort, and a sip near
  someone who ate or drank lately shares it like a meal, once a while.

Content comes from `Tools/_WG/Cooking/port_paragon_kitchen.py` (re-run it; don't edit `kitchen.yml`).

- **By hand**: bowls, cups, platters, rolling pins, pepper mills, pans, pots, kettles and teapots are built from the
  construction menu (`handcraft.yml`). A `wgChopRecipe` can carry a lucky `bonus` (a pearl from an oyster, roe from a fish).
  A kettle brews a quarter more for each quality step of what went in.
