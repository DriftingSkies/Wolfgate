# Ghost

The ghost orbit menu, in place of the upstream ghost warp window: a searchable list of everything a ghost can follow or
warp to, in two views the ghost switches between (the choice lasts the session):

- **By faction** (the default): players and ships under the faction they fly for, by their company's family (so the
  Federation's navy, civilians and high command are one faction), the unaligned last as Independent. Within a faction,
  players alive first, then critical, SSD and dead, then its ships; each tile keeps its status colour. After the
  factions: xenonids, stations and planetary sites, live events, then NPCs and ghosts, both folded. Admins also get
  antagonists on top.
- **By place**: everything under where it is: the sector first, then each world (surface, caves and orbit together),
  then anything in transit. Within a place, its stations and sites, live events, ships, then players by status. NPCs
  and ghosts keep their own folded sections.

Live events are the game loop's own first: warheads (with their arming state), planted nuclear drop beacons, Monolith
Scions (with their stage) and the Choir's flesh ships. Then singularities, tesla balls, rifts, anomalies and anything with
`GhostInterestComponent`; a nuke only while armed, and its disk only when it lies loose or someone carries it. For
ghosts, chat and radio messages also carry a link that orbits their sender.

**Ghost notices**, after SS13's ghost alerts: when something worth watching happens, every ghost gets a chip down the
right of the screen (the source's sprite, a heading, a line and a draining bar, 30 s by default; an anchor breach lasts 45 s and a drop beacon until it lands) and a chat line. Clicking either
orbits the source; right-click dismisses a chip. Sent for a parasite infection and a larva bursting, the Choir's song
offered, answered or turning someone Remade, a corpse offered at a Scion's Tap, a Scion climbing a stage, a flesh ship
launched at an anchor, a drop beacon planted, a warhead armed or counting down, and a primary anchor falling. The same
notice about the same source is held back for 20 s. Other systems call `GhostNoticeSystem.Notify`.

Entry points: `GhostOrbitWindow` and the client `GhostOrbitSystem` (the menu and the chat links), the server
`GhostOrbitSystem` (target list and orbit requests) with `GhostOrbitSystem.Grouping.cs` (faction, place and which events
are live), `GhostOrbitSystem.GameMode.cs` (the game loop's events), `GhostInterestComponent`, and the server and client
`GhostNoticeSystem` with `GhostNoticeChip`.
