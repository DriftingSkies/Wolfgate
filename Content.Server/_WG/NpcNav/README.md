# NpcNav

Fixes to how the NPC navmesh sees partial-tile structures, so cleanbots and other pathing NPCs walk round railings,
fences and barricades instead of grinding against them.

The navmesh samples each tile at 4x4 points, 1/8 of a tile in from each edge. A fixture thinner than a sample cell
(a railing is 0.065 thick at the tile edge, a fence 0.2 thick down the middle) can sit between those points and never
hit one, so the graph showed the tile as open. `WgThinFixtureBlocks` in `PathfindingSystem.ThinFixtures.cs` makes such
a fixture block every sample cell it overlaps. Fixtures at least a cell thick on both axes are sampled as before.

Door, access and climb flags used to carry over to every later sample point in a tile once one point hit such an
entity. A north-facing windoor or railing then split its tile's open floor into a quarter-tile strip and the rest,
and an NPC sent through the strip could not fit inside it. Flags now belong to the points the entity covers.
