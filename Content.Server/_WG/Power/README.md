# Power

High-draw power distribution. An APC can raise its output by only about 15 kW per 0.5 s power tick, and every load on
its network shares the shortfall, so one big machine switching on browns out everything beside it. Players stacked
APCs to add headroom. The LAPC substation (`LargeAPC`, from Mono) is the intended answer: HV in, LV out, 60 kW it can
add at once. This module makes that headroom real and lets capacitors raise it.

`WGNetworkBatteryUpgradeComponent` sets a network battery's `MaxSupply` and `SupplyRampTolerance` to its base values
times `1 + perRating × (rating - 1)` of one machine part (capacitors on the LAPC: 60, 120, 150 and 210 kW for ratings
1, 3, 4 and 6). The upgrade verb shows the bonus. The `WGLargeApc` guidebook page, linked under Power and from the
LAPC itself, tells players when to build one and how.

`WGLargeApcTest` pins the behaviour players see: a single APC carries a steady load past 15 kW by pass-through, a jump
past its headroom unpowers the other loads on the network, a second APC absorbs that jump, and a stock or upgraded LAPC
absorbs much larger ones.
