# AdminVessels

Admin-only vessels, spawned with the admin Spawn Vessel command and never sold (`purchasable: false`, group Custom).

The Lupercal (`WGLupercal`) is the admin station-ship: a ring-docked circle 97 tiles across with four docks, a
garden core (bridge, armory, quarters, admin machines round an atrium), an arboretum and a ring walkway, and four
departments between the spokes (Medical north-east, Commons and Science north-west, Cargo, Salvage and Vehicles
south-west, Power and Atmos south-east), each with a public inner side on the ring and a staff side on the working
ring. Its doors belong to ShipCommand zones (`WGShipZones` on the grid, `WGDoorAccessRule.zone` on each door). The
vessel adds `WGFtlUnrestricted`, `WGShipCommand`, the roster (`WGShipRoster`, callsign Lupercal), Mother Base barks
for its crew and counter staff (`WGCrewBarks`, with the three counter staff's own voices), `WGShipFitting` and its
player jobs (`WGShipJobs`).

It is built to hold. Every wall and window is an indestructible kind (`WallPlastitaniumIndestructible`, the bastions'
`WGLupercalWallDurasteel` in `walls.yml`, `PlastitaniumWindowIndestructible`, and `PlasturaniumWindowRadProofIndestructible`
round the reactor). The vessel's `ProtectedGrid` stops its floors being pried, laid over or reached by an RCD, and
`WGBlastproofFloors` (here, with a one-line hook in `ExplosionSystem.DamageFloorTile`) keeps blasts from breaking
them, though a blast still hurts what stands on them. Only the staff list and up open its maintenance panels or use
an emag aboard (ShipCommand's `StaffOnlyWires`), and no door aboard can be pried (`WGShipFitting.unpryableDoors`). The
core is entered through four glass gates on the garden paths, each two command doors interlocked by
`WGDoorInterlock` (Shared): neither opens unless the other is closed, so the pair can't hold each other open as the
device-link bolts before v0.29 could. They cycle like an airlock: someone the door would admit asking it to open while
its partner stands open has the partner close, and the door then opens for them. Two `WGLupercalSentry` turrets stand in each gate and fire only on whoever the
crew turn on (ShipCommand's `WGShipSentry`). Where a staff zone meets anywhere else, the door is a `WGHoloDoor`, a
hard-light barrier that parts for those its reader admits, closes 2 s behind them (`WGAutoCloseDoor`, since it has no
airlock to shut it) and can't be pried, hacked or broken; held open, its emitter
frame and a faint field stay lit (`Tools/_WG/AdminVessels/holodoor_open.py` draws the open states from the closed one).

The map is generated by the Shipwright scripts (`.agent_docs/wolfgate/shipwright/lupercal/draft/`, outside the
repo) and is draft v0.31: every room's machines, crew posts and counters for the roster, gun decks outside the
bastions, power from the zero-point generator and a shielded fusion reactor through one APC per area, atmospherics (a gas
plant with waste reclaim, distro and waste on their own pipe layers, vents, scrubbers and air alarms), firelocks,
fire alarms, cameras, signs and decals, PA speakers, extinguishers, lockers, repair tools, plumbed cryo pods, all nine
gases mined in their own chambers (oxygen and nitrogen feed the air, the other seven fill canisters), a washroom off
the lounge, planted gardens and glass observation wings either side of the north arm. Since v0.30: the core's south
room is a sauna, directional fans hold the air at every door to space and each core gate's outer door, wall art and
signs hang only on walls one space sees, and Science has an anomaly lab in R&D's old north end whose sealed chamber,
behind a blast door, takes what the generator makes: a `WGAnomalyChamberMarker` (`science.yml`; Shared
`WGAnomalyChamberComponent`) on a grid makes the generator spawn anomalies there (`AnomalySystem.Chamber.cs`, one
marked line in `AnomalySystem.Generator.cs`) rather than on a random tile of the ship. The salvage magnet faces space
and the station keeps its offers (`SalvageMagnetData` in the gameMap). Since v0.31 (the draft's `crewlife.py`): a
second paramedic and surgeon post, the NPC chef's post, pantry crate and serving spots on the kitchen's pass, the NPC
bartender's post behind the bar, open shutters in place of the pass's locked windoors so guests can take the chef's
food, a potted-plant vendor in hydroponics, and seventeen pride flags (PrideFlags) on walls of the public rooms and the
crew's own, by the wall-art rules (`crewlife.PRIDE_ROOMS`). The other machine kinds the vending survey found missing
don't fit any room without crowding it; `crewlife.VENDORS` places them once a room has space.

A fresh Lupercal stays put and defends: marines walk the posts, gunners defend the docks, the pilot holds station facing
the attacker (`WGShipRoster` on the vessel). Its crew are Elite, never run dry, are replaced two minutes after they
fall and turn to repairs on their own; two janitors work from Housekeeping. The ship spawns locked down to visitors:
the docks, the core, the bastions and every public zone are Locked until an admin opens them, while the staff zones
admit the staff list. The Docks zone covers the lobbies and arms; its doors are the three external airlocks across each
arm and the two docking airlocks at its tip. Its guns, hardpoints and sentries are indestructible with four times the
battery and recharge, its berth cranes run at the mobile crane's top speed, its shield powers itself, fires burn out
where they start (`WGNoFireSpread`), no round-start litter is strewn over it (`StationVariationHasRun`), and each fax
carries its room's name. Staff-side lockers follow a Staff lockers switch on the same console (a zone with no floor,
Staff by default); those on the public side are mapped unlocked. The shield is the ship's own
(`WGLupercalShieldGenerator`, in the admin machines room): the staff list and up open it and set its strength in the
generator's window. The vehicle bay sells heavy vehicles (`WGComputerVehicleShopLupercal`); its apron is public, and a
three-door gate opens it onto the Exchange lobby, wide enough for every vehicle sold there. `canisters.yml` holds the
admin gas stock: nine canisters that can't be destroyed, each a thousand times the usual gas at the usual pressure.
Its vending machines never run out (`WGShipFitting.unlimitedVending`) and sell what the sector's stations sell from
the same kind of machine and more (`vendingExtras`, the packs in `vending.yml`); `Docs/_WG/AdminVessels/vending-survey.md`
compares the stations' machines with the Lupercal's and lists the machines still to be placed by the generator.
Each drydock crane stands on a pad in the hull, open to space down its arm's lane; a wall with an external airlock seals
the pad from the room behind it, and the airlock carries the crane's high-voltage cable.

`crew.yml` holds the Lupercal's own people: the counter staff (Torvald Deepkeel the dockmaster in the north lobby,
who answers hails at the desk beside his counter, giving the bridge eight seconds to take a hail first; the desk,
`WGComputerLupercalDockDesk`, is the drydock booking service, so it outlives him, and a helm only officers and up may
sit at (`WGOfficerHelm`), since it stands in a public lobby; someone on a call there offers the caller the booking
window with a button in the hail window; Imre Kast the quartermaster at the Exchange, who sells fuel and refuels; Agathe
Lowe the apothecary at the Medical dock), their counter post markers, the `WGLupercalLawset`, and the silicons that
serve under it: the AI core in the admin machines room and the cyborg chassis with its Robotics spawn points.
`jobs.yml` holds the eleven player jobs (pilot, radio officer, gunner, dockmaster, marine, engineer, deckhand,
paramedic, surgeon, AI, cyborg), all closed until the owner opens them. `machines.yml` gives the debug generator,
thruster and gyroscope Lupercal ids, so a mapchecker run needn't whitelist debug parts.
