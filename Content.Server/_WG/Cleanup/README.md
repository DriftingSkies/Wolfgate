# Cleanup

Keeps Mono's cleanup systems (`Content.Server/_Mono/Cleanup`) safe and cheap at high entity counts.

- `CleanupHelperSystem` fails closed: a protection radius that is zero, negative or not finite keeps the entity.
- Player protection (`HasNearbyPlayers`) checks distances against a snapshot of player-bound entity positions,
  rebuilt at most once a second (`CleanupHelperSystem.Players.cs`), instead of a spatial lookup per candidate.
- `SpaceCleanupSystem` makes the same decisions as before but rejects candidates before pricing them whenever the
  answer doesn't depend on price (`SpaceCleanupSystem.Fast.cs`), and runs its wall-stuck overlap test through a
  compiled delegate instead of a reflection `Invoke` per contact.

`CleanupRadiusTest` and `CleanupPlayerSnapshotTest` cover it.
