# Farming

The botany rework: Stardew Valley-style farming laid over upstream botany for every plant holder, with machines that
turn the harvest into goods, animal care, and a market for it. The design and build history are in
[`Docs/_WG/Farming/DESIGN.md`](../../../Docs/_WG/Farming/DESIGN.md).

**Growth** (`WGFarmingSystem`): every 5 seconds each planted holder is checked. It ages only while lit and watered
(upstream water over 10). On a world with a day (a light cycle) it needs daylight, in the open or under a greenhouse roof, or a powered
`WGGrowLamp`, and its crop's outdoor temperature band unless roofed under a lamp; on stations and ships their lights
count, as do a bought ship's (`ShuttleDeedComponent`) landed on a world. Otherwise the system holds it with upstream's `SkipAging`, and examine says why. A seed's `wgCrop` entry
(`crops.yml`, keyed by seed ID) sets the real minutes of growing it takes; seeds without one keep upstream speed.
Unguarded outdoor crops on worlds are now and then pecked by birds unless a `WGScarecrow` is near.

**Quality**: a hand harvest (one marked hook in `PlantHolderSystem.DoHarvest`, raising `WGPlantHarvestedEvent`) rolls
normal, silver, gold or (with quality fertilizer) iridium per item from care and fertilizer; `WGProduceQualityComponent`
raises the price by 25%, 50% or 100%.

**Soil and water**: fertilizer items (`WGFertilizerComponent`) stay in the soil until the plant is gone: quality steps,
growth speed, or retaining soil. Sprinklers (`WGSprinklerComponent`) water the holders round them from their own tank.
Water collectors (`WGWaterCollectorSystem`) suit worlds, never wells: rain catchers fill while it rains on them,
moisture vaporators condense water on power (best when hot), snow melters melt snow and frost; each feeds the sprinklers
within six tiles.

**Processing** (`WGProcessorSystem`): one data-driven machine for every artisan machine (`recipes.yml`): keg (bottles
its own brew), preserves jar, cheese press, mayonnaise machine, loom, mill, composter, fertilizer barrel, nutrient mixer,
oak cask, glass kiln, seed maker, bee hive, mushroom log, compute rack, digester, ore washer, geode cracker and packing
table. A batch runs in real minutes while the machine is anchored and powered; goods keep the input's quality. A busy machine queues more of the same (`Queue`, up to `QueueCap`), taking the rest of a stack in one go, and runs them in turn.

**Market**: the packing table seals goods into TradeGoods crates (Luxuries, Supplies, Fabric, Fertilizer) that sell on
the live market. The order board (`WGOrderBoardSystem`) posts NPC orders from `orders.yml` and pays per item and on fill, set well above a chute or a packed trade crate; `excludeTags` keeps slices off whole-pie and cake orders.

**Animals** (`WGAnimalMoodSystem`): any animal that lays, gives milk or grows wool has a mood, raised by petting and a
fed belly, lowered by hunger; happy animals produce sooner and lay better eggs (a marked hook in `EggLayerSystem`).

**Crops from Paragon Moon**: `Tools/_WG/Farming/port_paragon.py` ports eleven crops from the owner's Paragon Moon
project (art CC BY-SA 3.0). Everything here is sold at the Homestead Supply vendor (`vendor.yml`), its structures as flatpacks a wrench unpacks
(`flatpacks.yml`).
