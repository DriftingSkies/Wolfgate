# CodaSites

Seven small places, and an echo of Central that goes by another name each round (Afterimage, The Hollow Hour...), far out in the Lattice's Coda (95 to 130 km). `WGCodaSiteSystem` places each one at round start
on every Lattice map, on a random bearing in the last band and clear of other grids (`wgCodaSite` prototypes in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WG/CodaSites/sites.yml`; admins: `wgcodasite place|where`). `Docs/_WG/CodaSites/DESIGN.md` has
the design and the decisions taken.

**Mercy.**
- **The Lighthouse**'s lamp sweeps a beam round the tower and blinks on radar up to 40 km off. Its keeper gives each
  visitor, once a round, a flare and a warm cup that calms 25 Drift. It shelters (a station anchor, `WGLatticeShelter`, no hazards) and calls "safe harbour" on common
  every ten minutes. Its keeper talks when players are near, sometimes asking after a dead player by name, and stands
  over anyone who wakes aboard.
- **The Last Anchor** can be fed power cells (a minute each) or exotic matter (three) to hold longer, or to catch
  again while failing. It holds for three minutes and fails for one by cutting its own power, and its lights go out with
  it. While it fails, players within 2 km take Drift.
- **The Hospice** is the planetside hospital, sealed against space with reinforced walls round its edge, its beds made
  into cots. An attendant speaks to each healed patient, who is marked and followed by whispers for a while. Its cots heal whoever lies on one, fully, for 25 Drift, and the third visit brings the Choir's song.

**Fear.**
- **The Orchard's vines** creep towards anyone near and snare anyone who stands still among them for four seconds; cutting one drops its hanged bodies as Remade.
- **The Graveyard Field's rocks** carry the round's dead (`WGCodaSiteSystem.DeadThisRound`); breaking a named one
  gives the nearest player its Drift, while tending one calms 10 Drift, once per grave.
- **The Waiting Room's board** calls a number every three minutes, spiking that player's Drift, until it's broken.
- **The Broadcast Tower** records the Common channel (never secure channels) and words spoken aloud at its mast, and
  replays them twenty minutes late as if on Common, with a speaker's name swapped and the numbers changed, until its
  mast is broken. Only players in the Coda's hazard bands receive the replays.

Every site's grid music is silence (`WGCodaSilence`), so no fallback music plays aboard. Shared stone, glass, light and clutter (`shared.yml`, `tiles.yml`) and what keeps a site standing (`WGCodaGridSystem`:
self-power, air that never drains, a hull that heals) are here too. The maps are built by `its generator (sites.py, kept outside the repo)`, with art from `its generator (draw_art.py, kept outside the repo)`, and tested by
`CodaSitesTest`.
