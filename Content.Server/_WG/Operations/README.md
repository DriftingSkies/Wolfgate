# Operations

Operations replace the random Solarian vault and Phaethon weapons cache events. A faction calls one in from the
Operations tab of its static uplink, paying a stake. The sector is warned three minutes ahead with the site's rough
position, then the site arrives at a random point 4 to 9 km from the sector's centre and at least 3 km from either
faction base (any grid with `WGOrdnanceStation`). It stays 20 to 25 minutes, then leaves with everything left on it.
Each faction runs one operation at a time, and each operation has its own cooldown after it ends.

- **PvP** (callable any time): the TSF's Vault Transfer and the PDV's Dynasty Arms Drop. Their payloads, the vault
  server and three arms crates, are contraband that pays the same in FMC or doubloons, so whichever faction turns
  them in at home is paid. A payload still on the site when it leaves, or destroyed, pays nobody.
- **PvE**: a risk ladder, from Routine (Dead Drop, Salvage) through Hazard, Assault and High-value to Extreme
  (Quarantine Breach), set out at the top of `operations.yml`. NPCs guard the site: those the map spawns and those the
  operation adds, one more for every `npcPerMembers` members of the caller's faction online.
  `guardFaction` puts every NPC and turret the site brings into one faction, so a map mapped with PDV guards (the
  Dug-in Garrison's vault) still fights a PDV caller. When all are down the
  caller gets its stake back plus the reward, and a spoils crate locked to its faction drops on the site; if the site
  leaves first, `failRefund` of the stake comes back. Higher rungs return more per credit staked, refund less and are
  announced to the whole sector more precisely; a test holds the first two in order.

**Planetside.** `planetChance` rolls a site onto a random built world instead of space (never Carcinoma,
`excludedWorlds`); 1 runs it only planetside (Bandit Den, Dug-in Garrison), and the uplink hides those while no world
is built. The ground is claimed when the operation is called: `WGPlanetPoiSystem.TryReserveOperationSite` finds fresh
ground clear of every site, road, cavern mouth, lake and landed grid, the way wrecks are placed, clears and pins it and
records it as a site with a named orbit signal, so the site never takes over a point of interest and expeditions and
contracts leave it alone. On arrival the grid (`planetGrids`, or `grids`) loads there, settled into the ground like a
wreck; when it leaves, the site's marker becomes an abandoned operation site. A planetside site gets `planetExtraTime`
more warning and stay for the hop down, and announcements name the world and round to `planetPrecision` metres.

Which jobs may call an operation is set on the operation (`jobs`); the uplink only lists what the buyer can call
now. The stock `BuyerJobCondition` doesn't work on static uplinks (it looks for a mind on the buyer), so the check is
`WGOperationReadyCondition`.

Entry points: `WGOperationSystem` (calls, placement, arrival, payouts), `WGOperationPrototype` (`wgOperation`),
`WGActiveOperationComponent`, `WGOperationReadyCondition`, the planet-site partial `WGPlanetPoiSystem.Operations`, and `Resources/Prototypes/_WG/Operations` (operations,
listings, payloads). Listings are written from the operations by `python Tools/_WG/Operations/listings.py`, so each
costs its operation's stake.
