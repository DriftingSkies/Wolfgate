# Pyramid

The Coda's rarest enemy: a chrome pyramid 24 m tall that flies and fights like a ship without being a
grid. Once a minute, each crewed ship in the Coda's hazard bands has a 0.3 % chance of drawing it (doubled at full
depth). That only starts 45 minutes into the round, at most one comes per round, and never while one is out.
`WGPyramidSystem` runs it; every number, including the rarity, is on the `WGPyramid` prototype in
`Resources/Prototypes/_WG/Pyramid/pyramid.yml`. Admins: `wgpyramid summon|collapse`; a summoned one hunts its grid whoever is aboard and lasers every other grid in range too.

- **Arrives** 650 m out with a warning to the crew, and shows on mass scanners as a red triangle (`RadarBlip`).
- **Hunts** the nearest crewed ship in the Coda within 1.5 km, circling it at 90 m and topping out at 16 m/s, so a fast
  ship can outrun it. With nothing to hunt for ten minutes it sinks away.
- **Barrages**: every 12 s within 320 m, it paints five three-tile patches on the hull, one under a crewmember, and
  speaks a protocol line.
  - The reticles close in over exactly six seconds, the length of the charge sound.
  - Crackling tethers reach from its eye to each mark, straightening and brightening as the charge builds.
  - Then lasers fire from the eye. Each hit lands like a heavy ship missile (intensity 150): an explosion of shattered
    chrome and broken pixels (`WGPyramidShatter`, no fire), with a shockwave, a flash and a moment of pixelated reality.
- **Force bubble**: ships within 35 m are shoved away, so it can't be rammed or boarded.
- **Warps reality**:
  - Space lenses around it (`SingularityDistortion`), and within 48 m reality swirls, ripples, tears into glitch bands
    and splits into its colours.
  - Every 10 s a wave runs out 400 m. As it leaves, the aura flares, the field spikes and a surge of fragments is
    flung upward; between waves, fragments peel off the faces and rise steadily.
  - The wave carries its ambience: each player hears it as the front reaches them, quieter farther out, along with a
    moment of warped screen, a camera jolt and 1.5 Drift.
- **Fights back**: 12,000 health, resists heat, cold and small arms, takes extra from shock, and mends itself after
  20 s unhurt. An EMP stalls it for 12 s.
- **Collapses**: for 10 s it groans, shudders harder and harder, flickers and drags its fragments back in. Then it
  folds flat, sends one last strong wave and leaves three chrome shards. Each sells for 12,000, and the Coda's hunters
  want them back (`WGCodaLure`).

The client side is in `Content.Client/_WG/Pyramid`: fragments, the reality field, impacts, tethers, lasers, waves and
warp, all toned down for reduced motion. The art comes from `its generator (draw_art.py, kept outside the repo)`, and the sounds come from
Paragon-Moon's chrome pyramid. `PyramidTest` covers hunting, barrages and the collapse.
