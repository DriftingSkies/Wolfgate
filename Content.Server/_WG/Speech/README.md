# Speech

Wolfgate speech accents. Neo-French is a thicker take on the upstream French accent: players pick it as the
`WGNeoFrenchAccent` speech trait, and admins can add `NeoFrenchAccent` to any speaking entity.

Entry points: `NeoFrenchAccentSystem` (handles `AccentGetEvent`: applies the `WGNeoFrench` word replacements, then
rewrites letters, some at random) and `NeoFrenchAccentComponent`.

`NonHumanoidGaspSystem` swallows the gasp sound of any breather that is not a humanoid, so critters that borrow a
humanoid emote set don't suffocate in a human voice.

Sound needs air. Someone who speaks aloud while the air around them is at or below hazard low pressure (20 kPa,
or no air at all) only whispers: `ChatSystem.WgSpeechMuffledByVacuum` (`ChatSystem.Vacuum.cs`) turns the speech into a
whisper just before it is sent, so it carries two tiles clearly and five muffled. What counts is the air at the
speaker's position (a locker or cryo pod counts its own), not their suit, so a hardsuit doesn't help. Radio is untouched, since it is sent before the check, and a language that needs no sound, such
as sign, is never muffled. `wg.speech.vacuum_whisper` switches the rule; the test pool turns it off, since test maps
have no air. Whispers strip markup permissively, so a stray `[` no longer throws and drops the message.
