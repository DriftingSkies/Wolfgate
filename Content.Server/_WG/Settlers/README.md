# Settlers

Settlers of Colossus, a board game of claiming the Colossus sector for one to four players. The boxed board
(`WGSettlersBoard`, 250 at the Wolfgate vendor) opens its window when used in hand, activated on a table, or through its
Play verb. Players take seats and anyone seated starts the game; everyone else within reach can watch. Leaving the
board or closing the window gives up the seat, but the player's pieces and cards stay in the game, and anyone may sit
in a vacant seat to carry it on. While a seat is vacant its turns pass and its discards and pirate moves are played at
random. A game pauses if every player leaves. Examining the box says when a game is being played on it, and its
guidebook page (`WGSettlers`) explains the rules. It sits in a Games section (`WGGames`) under Tables & References.

Rules: nineteen hex sectors (salvage yard, ice field, gas giant, farm world and ore belt, which yield scrap, ice, gas,
biomass and ore, plus one dead zone) carry the numbers 2-12 without 7; no 6 or 8 sits beside another. Setup places an
outpost and a lane per player, then again in reverse order; the second outpost pays one of each sector around it.
Each turn the player rolls two dice and every outpost on a sector with that number yields one card, a colony two,
unless the pirates sit there. On a 7, anyone holding more than seven cards discards half, and the roller moves the
pirates and robs a player beside them. Then the roller builds lanes (scrap and ice), outposts (scrap, ice, gas,
biomass), colonies (two biomass, three ore) and contracts (gas, biomass, ore), trades with the bank at 4:1 or through
a dock at 3:1 or 2:1, and trades with other players through table offers that always include the player whose turn
it is. Contracts: escort (move the pirates, counts toward the largest fleet), charter (a hidden point), survey (two
free lanes), windfall (two resources from the bank) and embargo (everyone hands over one resource). One contract may
be played a turn, not the turn it was bought. The longest route (5+ lanes) and the largest fleet (3+ escorts) are
worth two points each. Ten points on the player's own turn wins.

Entry points: `SettlersSystem` (server: window, seats, moves), and in `Content.Shared/_WG/Settlers` `SettlersGame`
(rules and moves), `SettlersState` (the board and the legality checks both sides run), `SettlersBoard` (the fixed hex
layout) and `SettlersRules` (every number). The client draws the board in `SettlersBoardControl` and the rest in
`SettlersWindow`.

The server sends each viewer the board through a UI message rather than a shared UI state, so everyone's hand and
contracts stay hidden from the others; the contract deck exists only on the server. A window asks for the board as
it opens.
