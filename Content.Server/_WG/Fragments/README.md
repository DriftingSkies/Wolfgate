# Fragments

Monolith fragments rebuilt around the Remade. A fragment is a rare random event (`WGFragmentEvent`, replacing Mono's
`MonolithFragmentSmallAppearance` in the monolithic events table) that drifts in on the line halfway between the TSF and
PDV bases (their anchored uplinks), at least 2.5 km from either and up to 2 km further off that line. Its Mono mobs are
swapped for Remade, and a fragment heart sits at its middle.

The fragment sends every announcement itself (`WGFragmentLine`, built by `WGFragmentText`), each with its own sound:
`seeing.ogg` as it falls in, a heartbeat as it lands, a hull creak before it leaves, a stinger when its heart breaks or
it gets away, and a storm wind once it leaves cold. The sender speaks in the Monolith's violet while the heart beats; the
text goes corpse white, flesh, then arterial red, and both fade to ash once the heart breaks. The landing, the warning
and the escape come through corrupted: Lattice noise, a garbled sender, hidden words written over real ones and bursts
that flicker live through the client's `[scramble]` tag; the landing and the escape are followed by a whisper. The
coordinates are never corrupted, and a world with no mass transceiver up misses these lines like any announcement.

Admins can force one with `addgamerule WGFragmentEvent` (any size), or a greater one with `addgamerule WGFragmentEventGreater`;
it lands 10 to 20 seconds after the first announcement.

It lands with a garrison of 8 Remade and 2 elites besides the mobs it swaps. While the heart stands, it sends a wave
every 120 seconds at the living players within 60 m, on the fragment or a ship alongside: four, plus one per two players,
an elite every other wave, at most 30 alive. Four in ten of a wave board a ship alongside: any grid a living player
near the heart is aboard, within 60 m of it, on tiles at least 6 m from every player. The rest land on the fragment, out
of sight where they can. When the fragment leaves, living Remade that boarded a ship leave with it; corpses stay. Nobody near, no wave. Breaking the heart
(3000 damage) stops the waves. Only someone standing on the fragment within 20 m of the heart can hurt it: blasts
never do, and harm from ship guns, ramming or anyone off the fragment is dropped, so a crew has to board. The hull
is sealed as the fragment lands (`SealHull`): its floors are blast-proof and every wall, door and window takes no
damage and stops blasts, so nothing fired from outside reaches in. Crews get in by prying its doors. Breaking it pays 500 to each faction's uplink, half again if members of both factions were aboard
at some point before it broke. Every player who was aboard or within 60 m of the heart while it stood, whatever their
faction, also gets 250,000 thalers, untaxed, in the bank of the character they took part as, so outside crews have a
reason to help.
The fragment leaves after 20 minutes, with a warning 5 minutes before.

**Greater fragments.** One grid, `monolithfragmentlarge`, is a greater fragment (`sizes` in the rule): a whole shard of the
Monolith with its own announcements and name, a garrison of ninety waiting as it lands (75 and 15 elites), far heavier and quicker waves (14 a wave,
one more per player, an elite every wave, every 75 seconds, up to 150 alive), a heart twice as hard (6000), more decor, monolith crystal growing through it and the crew it took on its way in,
and two and a half times the faction reward with 650,000 to each participant. The grid is picked as the event is added,
so its first announcement already knows its size. Every heart sings the Coda's song to players nearby (`CodaSong`).

The Remade here belong to no Monolith, so nothing grows, climbs or turns corpses. A fragment never holds a Scion, a
splinter or any other `WGMonolith`.

Tuning is in `Resources/Prototypes/_WG/Fragments/fragments.yml`; the logic is `WGFragmentRuleSystem`.
