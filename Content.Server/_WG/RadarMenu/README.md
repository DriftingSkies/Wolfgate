# RadarMenu

A left click on a helm, cockpit or mass scanner radar opens a console-styled menu for the spot, contact or waypoint
under the cursor. A click on a world still lists its signals, and radars whose clicks aim guns (gunnery, drones, the
cockpit's aim) keep their clicks. Only an anchored helm or mass scanner has the menu: the admin radar, an admin ghost's
scanner and handheld scanners don't.

- **A spot:** autopilot to here, save a waypoint here.
- **A contact:** autopilot alongside (it stops 30 m clear of the hull, on our side), dock at…, hail, save a waypoint
  on it. The heading shows the contact's name as the radar labels it, with range and bearing.
- **A waypoint:** autopilot to it, delete it.
- **A world:** list its signals (what a click on a world did before), autopilot into orbit, save a waypoint on it.
  The autopilot heads for a point three quarters into the world's orbit range on our side and, once the ship is in
  range, enters orbit through `WGOrbitEntrySystem.TryEnterOrbit`, as the console's orbit button does; an unsanctioned
  world asks first. Radars without the menu still list a clicked world's signals at once.
- **Always:** copy coordinates, recentre a panned radar, and at a helm, cancel the autopilot.

Flying, docking, hailing and cancelling need a helm; waypoints need a helm or mass scanner on a grid. Keys a radar can't
use are greyed out.

**Dock at…** asks the server which free docks on the contact the ship can dock with and reach by a clear lane
(`WGAutoDockOptionsRequestMessage`), and lists them nearest first, with **Any free dock** on top. The ship then flies
itself in exactly as the NPC boarding pilots do, with their planner (`WGPilotDutySystem.TryPlanDock`) and docking
settings: out to a standoff 40 m in front of the port, settle there turned to dock, creep in port to port, and dock
once the ports line up, as the Dock key does. Nothing moves the ship but its own steering. A bump or a stall goes back
out to the standoff and tries again with a fresh plan; after three tries, or if the target is gone, or someone takes
the controls, it stops and the pilot is told why. A ship that is already docked must undock first.

Entry points: `WGAutoDockSystem` (server), `ShuttleNavControl.RadarMenu.cs` and `WGRadarContextMenu` (client); the
click reaches the menu through the radar's existing click handling in `ShuttleNavControl.PlanetSignals.cs`.
`WGAutoDockTest` covers listing, the approach, settle and creep, docking once lined up, and giving up.
