# Fishing

Fishing on planets and outposts (`WGFishingSystem`). A fishing rod used on a planet's standing water (its biome's lakes and ponds, `BiomeSystem.WgIsWater`) or on a built fish pond casts the line. After a wait (shorter with bait) a fish bites and the angler has 2.5 seconds to reel in by using the rod: too early scares it off, too late lets it go, and walking off snaps the line. What bites follows the world's outside temperature (`wgFishTable`: frozen, cool, warm, hot, and a pond table), and the catch rolls a quality, better with bait (worms from foraging, dough balls chopped from dough). Fish are chopped into fillets or shellfish meat on the Cooking prep board, then fried, baked or stewed. Content comes from `Tools/_WG/Fishing/port_fishing.py` (fish from Paragon Moon and Meridian, art CC BY-SA 3.0).
