# CodaSong

The song the Coda sings in a drifting mind. Each player hears their own: `WGCodaSongSystem` (client) plays every part
of a piece at once, clean and turned, and mixes them live from the listener's Drift.

- **Low Drift** (from 8): a lone soprano, faint and far off. More voices join, nearer, as Drift climbs, the profondo last.
- **From 55**: the parts turn one by one (metal, whisper, a vast throat, a tritone off), the soprano last.
- **90 and above**: the turning stops and the clearest piece, the Canon, plays: the finality.
- **Ghosts in the Coda's hazard bands** have joined the choir: they hear every voice, clear and close.
- **A fragment's heart** sings to anyone within 60 m (300 m for a greater fragment), whatever their Drift, its singers turning as
  it is hurt (`WGSongSourceSystem`), until it breaks.
- **A Pyramid wave** passing through the listener turns every singer for a few seconds.
- **Ambient music** crosses over with it both ways: a piece starting fades the ambient track out as it fades in, and
  an ambient track starting while a piece plays fades the song out; the song comes back once that track ends. A
  Lattice band's own piece always plays first.
- **In a fight** the song blends under the combat music instead of holding it off. The more hurt the listener, the
  further the choir sinks and the more its singers turn; once they are critical, every voice falls away but the
  soprano, who sings on, clear.

Pieces end in silence and the next comes after 20 to 60 seconds (6 to 15 near a heart), so no two listeners hear the
same mix. Pieces are `wgCodaSong` prototypes in `Resources/Prototypes/_WG/CodaSong/songs.yml`; their stems are rendered
by `python its generator (make_hymn.py, kept outside the repo) --variant <name> --stems Resources/Audio/_WG/CodaSong/<name>`, the same
generator that renders whole pieces (`--list` names them).
