# Foraging

Foraging on planets (`WGForagingSystem`). Every minute each planet world with players on it grows up to two forage spots near a random player there, on open, dry, unblocked ground, up to 24 a world. Which spots grow follows the world's outside temperature (`wgForageTable`): berries, wild strawberries, brambles, mushrooms, onions, poppies, sunflowers, tea, coffee, rocknuts and worm casts. A spot is picked by hand after a two-second gather and gives its loot, sometimes seeds of crops the shops don't sell; unpicked spots wither after 25 minutes. Spots and tables are generated by `Tools/_WG/Fishing/port_fishing.py`.
