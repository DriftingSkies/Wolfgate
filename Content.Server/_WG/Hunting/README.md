# Hunting

Game animals on the planets, ported from the owner's Paragon Moon project (art CC BY-SA 3.0), and what they butcher
into. Prey (saiga, wild goats, badgers, raccoons, giant moles) bolts from anyone close, using Verda's skittish behaviour;
predators (wolf and hyena packs, bobcats, direbears, big rats, mossbacks) hunt whoever they see. The game is added to
the planets' wildlife tables by climate: wolves, goats and saiga on Thrascias' tundra and boreal forest, woodland game
and the rare direbear on Verda, hyenas and moles in Merak's dunes and badlands, big rats in Fervidus' ash, mossbacks on
the shores. Its entries sit between `WOLFGATE-HUNTING` comments in those Planets files.

A carcass butchers with a knife (upstream `Butcherable`) into venison, game, wolf or bear meat, a pelt or hide, and
bones. Game meat chops into mince for stews and fries or bakes into steaks (Cooking); the smoking rack makes jerky and
dried fish, the tanning rack leather, and the nutrient mixer grinds bones into quality fertilizer (Farming). Leather
packs into Fabric crates and the order board asks for pelts and jerky.

The animals, meat and recipes are data, written by `Tools/_WG/Hunting/port_hunting.py`; `WGHuntingSystem` grades the cuts by how clean the kill was (a marked hook in `SharpSystem` raises `WGButcheredEvent`). Re-run it after changing its
tables; don't edit `hunting.yml` or the marked table entries by hand.
