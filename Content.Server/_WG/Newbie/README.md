# Newbie

Marks new players so others can go easy on them. A player whose total playtime is under `wg.newbie.hours` (8 by
default, 0 turns it off) gets a learner plate over their head and the examine line "They are new around here." on
whatever body they control. A ship whose owner (`ShipOwnershipComponent`) is new, or that a new player is piloting, has "(L)" after its name on radar
and in the hail list and screen. A ship hiding its label shows no tag.

Players can hide their own tag with "Show my newbie tag" in Options > Extra (`wg.newbie.show_tag`, a replicated client
cvar). A hidden tag means the newbie rules don't cover them. The tag clears by itself once they pass the threshold.
Admins still see the ⏲ mark in the ahelp list, which uses the separate `admin.new_player_threshold`.

The rules that go with the tag are Conflict Rule 6 (`WGRuleConflictSixNewPlayers`), linked from the Monolith ruleset.
A trait with `newbieBlocked: true` (Heretic, which makes its holder a kill target) is hidden in a tagged player's
character editor and skipped by `TraitSystem` when they spawn.

Once per join, a new player gets a welcome popup: a short introduction, a button to the rule and "Don't show this
again" (`wg.newbie.show_welcome`, client only). A player under `wg.newbie.fresh_hours` (1 by default) then gets a
second popup telling them to join a crew before buying a ship. It can't be turned off and stops after that hour.
`wg.newbie.welcome` turns both off server-wide, and the test pool keeps it off.

Entry points: the server `WGNewbieTagSystem` adds and removes `WGNewbieComponent` (bodies) and
`WGNewbieShipComponent` (ships) when a player attaches and every two seconds after that. Bodies detached from a
player lose the tag, while ships keep theirs while the owner is offline. The shared `WGNewbieSystem` writes the
examine line and `TagShipName`, which the IFF label and `HailingSystem` call. The server `WGNewbieWelcomeSystem` sends
`WGNewbieWelcomeEvent` and the client one of the same name opens the popups. The client `WGNewbieIconSystem` draws
the icon. `Tools/_WG/Newbie/gen_newbie_icon.py` draws the plate.
