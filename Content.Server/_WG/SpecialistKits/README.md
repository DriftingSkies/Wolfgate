# SpecialistKits

A specialist rack at the Halcyon (in the marines' bunk room) and at Helios (by the Vanguard outfitters) hands each
player one free kit per round, from RMC's automated vendor. TSF kits are Marine and Navy jobs, named with their MOS or
rating; PDV kits are the same roles under Ottoman names.

| Group | TSF | PDV | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infantry | Assaultman (0351) | Lağımcı | Elite |
| Infantry | Antitank Missileman (0352) | Darbzenci | Elite |
| Infantry | Machine Gunner (0331) | Yeniçeri | Line |
| Infantry | Mortarman (0341) | Humbaracı | Line |
| Infantry | Scout Sniper (0317) | Tüfekçi | Elite |
| Infantry | Reconnaissance Man (0321) | Akıncı | Line |
| Combat support | Combat Engineer (1371) | Cebeci | Engineer / Artificer job |
| Combat support | Corpsman (HM) | Cerrah | Corpsman / Jarrah job |
| Combat support | Master-at-Arms (MA) | Bostancı | Elite |
| Combat support | LAV Crewman (0313) | Sipahi | Cavalry |
| Support | Culinary Specialist (CS) | Aşçı | Open |
| Support | Aviation Machinist's Mate (AD) | Arabacı | Open |
| Support | Landing Support Specialist (0481) | Menzilci | Open |

Limits are per kit and per faction, by members online: elite 1, plus 1 at 20, up to 2; cavalry 1; line 1, plus 1 per
10, up to 3. They are `membersPerSlot` and `maxSlots` on each kit's `WGSpecialistKit`. RMC's own `sharedSpecLimit`
counts by entry position across every rack, so it would mix the factions' kits; the limit is checked instead on
`WGSpecialistKitVendAttemptEvent`, which a marked hook in `SharedCMAutomatedVendorSystem` raises.

Each kit is a case (`WGKitCaseBase`) whose `StorageFill` is its gear. `WGTakeOnlyStorage` seals it once filled, so
nothing goes back in, and `WGKitCaseSystem` deletes it once empty. The uplink's replacement listing for a kit sells the
same case, to a player who drew that kit this round (`WGKitHolderCondition`), once per kit per player per round (a
`WGListingCooldownCondition` keyed by the listing, with a cooldown longer than a round, tracked by account). Eight kits carry an implant autosurgeon;
it has no purchase mark, so the implant ends with the round. A kit whose implant needs a cybernetic arm or an organic
body names that in its `warning`. The cavalry kits carry a Gygax voucher (`cavalry.yml`) that sets down a powered
mech when used in hand.

In the rack (`WGKitContents`, through marked hooks in RMC's vendor UI), clicking a kit's row opens a drop-down of its
gear and the Take button draws it. The row shows the slots left, from `WGSpecialistVendorComponent`, which the server
recounts every two seconds, or the job a kit is held to. The section header shows the pick as taken/total.

Entry points: `WGSpecialistKitSystem`, `WGKitCaseSystem`, `WGSpecialistKitComponent` on each kit, and
`Resources/Prototypes/_WG/SpecialistKits` (kits, cavalry, vendors, replacements).
