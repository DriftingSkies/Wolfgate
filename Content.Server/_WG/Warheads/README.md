# Warheads

Nuclear warheads with real payloads. A warhead (`WGWarhead`) counts down, plays Skyrat's alarm and voiced
countdown in its last 10 seconds, floats a green countdown over itself, and then detonates its payload
(`wgWarheadPayload`): a blast, an immediate radiation burst, decaying fallout that leaves a green haze, an EMP, and
for the client a white-out flash, a shockwave ring that bends the screen and shakes the camera, and a procedural
fireball in space or a rising mushroom cloud on a planet. A strategic payload deletes the grid it sits on outright,
never a planet. Every payload's `eraseRadius` wipes ground zero before the blast (`WGWarheadSystem.Erase.cs`): every
physical thing there, everything on a ship's grid, decals and floor tiles (a planet keeps its ground), so no paint or
fittings are left floating. Fallout is lethal: 15-40 rad/s at its centre, halving every 5-12 minutes.

Warheads are admin and antag only for now. Chassis:

- `WGWarheadTactical`: hand cart, timer or remote fuze (old station nuke sprite).
- `WGWarheadField`: Skyrat's nuke, timer plus two keys.
- `WGWarheadStrategic`: cmss13's 64x64 device, two keys, deletes its grid.
- `WGWarheadScuttleTerminal`: Skyrat's terminal, two keys or a remote signal.
- Mono's Atlant missile now detonates `WGPayloadMissile` on impact.
- `WGWarheadTacticalKeyed`: the tactical chassis with a keyhole (`WGWarheadKeyLock`): it arms only with its own
  `WGNukeKey` in. Factions buy it through the Ordnance module.

Clicking a warhead opens its control panel (`WGWarheadWindow`): hazard stripes, a radiation trefoil that spins
while it counts, status lamps, a seven-segment timer, and a section per fuze it has: timer presets and a covered
start button, two key cylinders with the second key's window draining, a safety lever for the remote fuze, and a
payload readout (rough blast radius, flash, fallout, EMP, grid deletion).

Arming mechanisms are separate components: `WGTimerFuze` (set a timer, start the countdown), `WGTwoPersonArming`
(two different people with access turn keys within a few seconds), `WGRemoteFuze` (take the safety off, then a
signal on the Detonate port starts a short countdown; link a remote signaller or a `WGDeadManSwitch`), and
`WGImpactFuze` (goes off when triggered).

**Defusing.** A multitool no longer disarms a warhead (`DisarmQuality` is null unless a prototype sets one). Unscrew its
panel and cut one of two fuze wires in the stock wires window, repainted as a fuzing assembly
(`WGNuclearWiresBoundUserInterface`, `WiresMenu.Nuclear.cs`, wire layout `WGWarheadFuze`). One wire
(`WGWarheadDisarmWireAction`) makes it safe, even in the final seconds; the other (`WGWarheadDetonateWireAction`) sets
it off. Colours are random per warhead, so it is a coin flip. Cutting a wire on a safe warhead does nothing.

The station nuke uses Skyrat's sprites and alarm, and `WGNukePayload` gives it the countdown display, the voice and
the detonation effects on top of its own blast (one marked call in `NukeSystem.ActivateBomb`).

Admins build bespoke warheads in the nuke designer (`nukedesigner`, or Nuke Designer in the Wolfgate admin tab):
start from a payload preset, edit every field, pick a chassis, fuzes and a name, then spawn it or set the payload off
where you stand.

Every nuclear detonation raises `WGNuclearDetonatedEvent` (where, and a kill radius of twice the erase radius plus 16 m) before
it does anything, so a Monolith's Prime can be destroyed by it; arming raises `WGWarheadArmedEvent`, which DEFCON follows.

Entry points: `WGWarheadSystem` (countdowns, detonation, arming, designer spawns), the client's
`WGWarheadEffectsSystem` with its overlays and the `nuclear_blast` and `fallout_haze` shaders, and `WGNukeDesignerEui`.
