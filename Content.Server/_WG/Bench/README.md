# Bench

The Common Writ, the sector's in-character law, and the tools Caelestinus judges use to enforce it. The laws are
`wgLaw` prototypes grouped by `wgLawChapter` (`Resources/Prototypes/_WG/Bench/laws.yml`); the Common Writ page
(`WGCommonWrit`, under Caelestinus in the lore book) and the app both read them. Judges spawn with `WGBookCommonWrit`,
a book that opens that page. Items listed in the Writ's Schedule of Unlawful Material carry `Contraband` with the
`WGUnlawful` severity, so examine marks them. `Docs/_WG/Bench/LAWBOOK.md` is the review copy.

**Judges.** A judge or the Overseer (`SecurityGuard`, `StationRepresentative`: `WGBenchSystem.JudgeJobs`) spawns with the Bench app on their PDA. A
`WGBenchTerminal` gives the same tools in place. Ticket tab: pick someone within `wg.bench.range` (3 tiles, plus 1.5 so someone stepping back stays listed and can be ticketed), tick laws
and set each fine inside its range, add posted ordinances or custom charges, then Issue. Ledger tab: the round's
tickets, and Pardon. Laws tab: the Writ, plus ordinances judges post for the round. Only judges can act; anyone else
holding the app only reads the laws.

**Fines.** `WGBenchSystem.Issue` takes what the offender's bank holds into the NFSD sector account, hands the judge a
stamped `WGBenchTicket` paper and writes a line to their sector criminal record. The rest is Bench debt for the round:
`WGBenchSystem.Fines.cs` watches `BalanceChangedEvent` and takes `wg.bench.garnish` (half) of each later gain. Debt
past `wg.bench.deadline_minutes` (30) or over `wg.bench.debt_cap` (50,000) sets the record Wanted until it is paid.
An Outlawry charge carries no fine: Wanted at once and a `WGShipbreakerWarrant` bounty (`wg.bench.outlaw_bounty`).
Soft limits stop a ticket being used to grief: each law or ordinance counts once per ticket, at most
`WGBenchMath.MaxCharges` (8) charges, custom fines up to `MaxCustomFine` (100,000), and `WGBenchMath.Cap` holds a
ticket to `wg.bench.ticket_cap` (200,000) and a person to `wg.bench.round_cap` (400,000) a round, pardons excluded.
A pardon refunds everything taken, drops the debt and lifts what the ticket caused. Everything resets at round end.

`WGBenchMath` (Shared) holds the sums and is unit-tested; `WGBenchTest` covers the bank, garnish, overdue, outlawry and
pardon flow. Impound and banishment are recorded on the ticket and enforced by judges in character.

**Judge ship refits** touch hardpoints only. The Roswell's port bow hardpoint carries `WGBenchHarpoonGun`, an ARACHNE
hacking harpoon whose `WGBenchHarpoon` draws a fresh dart two minutes after each shot (`WGBenchHarpoonSystem`), in
place of a Cerberus. The Lightning swaps its starboard outer TOVEK for a SHRIKE ion disruptor (`WGIonDisruptor`) to
stop ships that won't heave to.
