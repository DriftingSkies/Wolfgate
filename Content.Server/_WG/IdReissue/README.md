# IdReissue

A player who lost their ID asks the shipyard dealer on Caelestinus Central ("I lost my ID."). He quotes the fee
(500 by default) and which ship deeds he can move, and on a yes takes it from the cash on the counter or else straight
from their bank, since they have no card to put down; an Ironman pays cash. The new card has the old one's name, job,
faction, access and crew record key, and its ColoTalk number, contacts and messages (NanoChat `TransferCard`). Because
ship doors, allow lists and crew lists key on that record, it opens whatever the old card opened. It comes in a PDA
like the one the lost card sat in. Every lost card, wherever it is, is replaced by an inert `WGVoidIdCard`: no ID at
all, so no shipyard, trader, door or console takes it.

Ship deeds move only for the player's ships docked to the dealer's station or within 150 m of it, and every ID card
holding those deeds loses them, a thief's copy included. The deed of a ship further out goes with the voided card and
the system remembers it until the ship is brought back and the player reissues again; a hijacker who flew off with it
keeps flying it, but the stolen card buys and opens nothing. A ship is the player's when one of their cards holds its
deed, when its deed went with a card they voided, or when it is registered to their account under this character's
name, which covers a card that was destroyed. A card destroyed mid-round is remembered as it was last, so it reprints
with its current job and ColoTalk number. A player carrying a valid card of their own is refused, so a card is never
doubled, and a character waits 15 minutes between reissues (the component's `cooldown`).

An owner turned away at a door of their own ship without a valid card of theirs gets a popup pointing them to the
dealer, at most every 30 seconds.

Entry points: `WGIdReissueSystem` (`TryQuote` and `Reissue`, and `Remember`, which keeps each character's ID as seen at
spawn so a destroyed card can still be reprinted), its `Trader` partial (the `ReissueId` dialogue action on any trader
with `WGTraderIdReissueComponent`, which holds the fee and range) and `DoorHint` partial, and the `WGVoidIdCard` prototype.
Cards are matched to a character by the `IdCardOwnerComponent` stamp from the Traders module.
