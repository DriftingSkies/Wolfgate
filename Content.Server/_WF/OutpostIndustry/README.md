# OutpostIndustry

Producers an outpost runs for income (outposts plan F10.2). Each is a gizmo from the outpost outfitter, set up from
its pack, and runs only anchored on an outpost, powered, while someone is home (`WFRaidDirectorSystem.SomeoneHome`)
and while the outpost's producers together are under the hourly cap (`wf.outposts.producer_cap`, 4000 spesos of value;
`WFOutpostOutputComponent`, round state). Examining one says why it is idle.

- Parts press (`WFPartsPressComponent`): once a minute turns 5 steel and 2 plastic sheets from its material storage
  into a box of pressed machine parts (150 spesos).
- Power exporter (`WFPowerExporterComponent`): draws 30 kW and earns 0.5 spesos a second as credit, saved with the
  outpost; a builder cashes it out with the Cash out verb.
- Mining bot dock (`WFMiningBotDockComponent`): makes two mining bots (`WFMobMiningBot`), a new one 5 minutes after one
  is lost, and stores what they bring. A bot (`WFMiningBotComponent`, `WFMiningBotOperator` in `WFMiningBotCompound`)
  walks to the open tile beside the nearest ore-bearing rock within 24 m of the dock, drills it for 3 s, picks up the
  ore and goes home with 8 pieces or when none is left; in the Active window or while the dock is idle it rests at
  the dock. Bots are mobs, so not saved: a loaded dock makes them again, and a packed one takes them away.

Entry points: `WFOutpostIndustrySystem` (gate, cap, press, exporter) and `WFOutpostIndustrySystem.MiningBots`.
