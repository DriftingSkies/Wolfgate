# Fighters

Small craft a pilot flies directly, the first step towards Starsector-style carriers and fighter wings. A fighter is
its own entity, not a mech: alt-click it and pick Board to climb in. Its cockpit is pressurised with standard air,
so the pilot needs no suit. Once in, the pilot is on the shuttle keys: thrust and strafe relative to the nose, turn on
the rotate keys, hold brake to stop. Combat mode switches on, the view zooms out, and the fighter's flight deck takes
over the screen: instruments and controls on the left, the world view and chat in the middle, a radar scope on the
right. It is the ship cockpit's look, built from its parts but owned by the fighter (`FighterCockpitView`); EXIT or
the hotbar's Fighter cockpit action closes it.

Flight modes follow the ship console: Cruise keeps every bit of velocity, Drive (the default) bleeds off motion along
any axis nobody is thrusting on, and Park ignores thrust and brakes to a stop while still letting the nose turn.
Follow cursor turns the fighter to face the cursor in place of the rotate keys. Engine burns and RCS puffs show on the
sprite whenever a thruster fires, drive's dampening included. The nose gun fires within 8° of the nose, aiming along
the edge of that cone when the cursor is wider, and never while over a grid. A cell holds one burst and charges
nothing while the gun is firing; once it runs dry the gun stays locked until the cell is full again. Under gravity it only taxis, and an empty fighter brakes to a stop.

- `WFFighterBroadsword`, the Broadsword heavy fighter: 1.5 tiles, 3000 damage behind heavy mech armour, 80 m/s top
  speed. Its gun fires the AC-35 chaingun's `35mmBulletAP` at the AC-35's rate, speed and spread: a 2 s burst of
  28 rounds, then 3 to 4 s to refill (self-recharge steps once a second). The gunshot plays at most every 0.3 s so the copies do not drown each other.

Entry points: `FighterComponent` (handling numbers, flight mode, pilot slot, burst lock) and `SharedFighterSystem`
(boarding, input, gun rules) in Shared; `FighterSystem` (cockpit air, flight deck state, cursor aim, burst unlock)
and `FighterMovementController` (flight) in Server; `FighterSystem` (input context, cursor stream) and
`FighterCockpitView` in Client. Fighters are admin-spawned for now. Not here yet: carriers, launch bays, remote
control from a console, and Escape to leave the flight deck.
