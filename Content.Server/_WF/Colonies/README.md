# Colonies

World development: outposts grow their planet into a colony that trades with the sector, then into a company's charter
world with an orbital yard. The design and the step order are in `Docs/_WF/Colonies/DESIGN.md`. Behind
`wf.colonies.enabled` (on).

**The index.** Every holder (a company, `c<id>`, or a player without one, `u<guid>`) scores points on each world from
its outposts there: what its buildings put on the shelf, crates hauled to the colony exchange, raids beaten or broken
in, and a trickle for each hour home. Scores lose 5% a day and are
saved in `wolfgate_world_development`. A world's total sets its tier: Settled 100, Colony 500, Charter 2,000,
Dominion 6,000 (`ColonyCVars`), with 10% slack on the way down. The Development tab on the outpost console
(`WFColonyDevelopmentPanel`) shows the tier, the holders and their shares, and the viewer's output.

**The colony station.** At Colony tier the world's sector body (or its ground, with none) becomes a runtime ledger
station (`WFColonyComponent`, `WFColonySystem.Station.cs`) that uses Supplies and Medical. Outposts on the world trade
on its shelf (`WFTradeMarketLinkComponent`). Buildings on outposts run its industries and goods for their holder
(`wfColonyBuilding`, `colonies.yml`): hydroponics trays, penned livestock, ore silos, the
parts press, and extractors over the uranium, volatile and biomass deposits each world gets (`wfColonyWorld`, which
also gives a world its `baseTier` and the `rates` its kinds run at). A `climate: true` kind grows at its full rate
between 5 and 30 C outside and falls to 40% over the next 30 degrees. An entity counts
for every kind it matches. An animal counts only alive and fed, and an ore silo makes Metals out of the raw ore in it
(`worksMaterials`), which runs down. The colony pays each holder for its share of the output, grows its people up to its
housing (beds and cryopods), and posts colony contracts when it runs short. What the sector owes it comes down as
import drops at a trade hub (`WFColonySystem.Imports.cs`), stopped by a blockade. The colony exchange vendor and chute
(outfitter packs) sell off and buy onto the one shelf. The chute also buys produce (`wfColonyProduce`,
`WFColonySystem.Produce.cs`): crops, eggs, milk, wool and hides are paid for in cash at their appraised worth and go on
the shelf as that worth of Supplies or Fabric, scoring for the seller's holder.

**Supply runs.** With `wf.colonies.supply_runs` and encounters on, what a colony on the sector map is owed comes by
freighter (`WFColonySystem.Supply.cs`): a `WFEncounterColonySupply` ship flies to the world, holds over it and drops a
pod at a ready trade hub every 30 s until its hold is empty. A run shot down loses what it carried. Otherwise the timed
drops stand.

**The seat.** A world built at Settled tier or past gets the seat its tier has reached among its sites (`wfColonySeat`,
`seats.yml`, `WFColonySystem.Seat.cs`, added through `WFPlanetPoiExtraSitesEvent`): a landing field, then a depot with
the exchange and a public trade hub, then the governor's hall. A depot keeps the colony standing with no outpost on the
world, its bunks house people, and some of the colony's people live there as colonists; one killed costs the colony
`wf.colonies.people_per_colonist` people. The maps are generated by `Tools/_WF/Colonies/seats.py`. A world that tiers up
mid-round gets its new seat at the next round. `wf.colonies.seats` turns seats off (the test pool does).

**Territory.** The Colonies driver of sector control holds a Colony world's cell as the neutral colonies faction and a
Charter world's as its governor (`WFColonySystem.Territory.cs`). Someone home keeps the cell; a parked ship can't
retake it. A Reaver hold next door blockades the colony and raises DEFCON.

**The charter.** At Charter tier the largest company with three members and 35% of the points governs
(`wolfgate_world_charter`, `WFColonySystem.Charter.cs`). A rival with a quarter more points for three daily checks takes
it; under 20% it lapses. Grants, successions and lapses are announced as writs of the Bench, and Article V of the
Common Writ (Bench) makes claim jumping and interdicting a supply run offences. While the war is hot the daily checks
count for no one and a chartered colony draws `wf.colonies.war_draw` times its Supplies and Medical. The governor's
Charter card, on the charter office terminal in the hall (`WFColonySystem.Office.cs`) or on the Development tab while
no hall stands, sets the orbital batteries
(`WFOrbitalDefenceSystem` in OutpostRaids) on campaign ships, closes the world to founders without a permit, grants
and revokes permits, and at Dominion opens yard berths to other companies.

**The yard.** At Dominion the governor requests a yard platform kit (dropped at their hub once a round) and deploys it
in the world's orbit (`WFColonyYardSystem`). The platform is saved by world in `wolfgate_world_yard` and placed again
each round. Its console lays down `wfYardDesign` builds (`yard.yml`) of hulls no shipyard sells, for the governor or a
berth holder, two at a time; each stage takes Ship Components and Metals crates at the yard chute, then crane time.
A build stands by the platform as a frame (`WFColonyYardSystem.Frame.cs`): its deck goes down from the keel outward
over the first 40% of the work, then its hull goes up, with the platform's cranes working the newest piece. The
finished vessel is loaded in the frame's place.
Builds are saved in `wolfgate_yard_job`. A finished hull is deeded to a company member on the map (or registered to the
company), set to company access, and its first completion grants the company the fleet licence.

**Heritage.** A round counts for an outpost when someone is home long enough (`WFColonySystem.Heritage.cs`): 5 rounds
lower its reload markup, 15 add two cryopods and a quarter to its producer cap, 30 lower the markup again and name it on
the planet map. A sale resets it. From 5 rounds an outpost also comes down on the lot it last stood on when that lot is
free.

**Reavers.** The Reaver campaign's spread weighs a cell beside a colony `wf.colonies.reaver_draw` times
(`WFReaverSpreadWeightEvent`), so strongholds grow towards colonies.

Entry points: `WFColonySystem` (scores, tiers, `CheckCharter`, the station), `WFColonyYardSystem` (`FoundYard`,
`Demolish`, `TryOrder`, `TryFeed`, `Work`), `WFColonyDevelopmentPanel`, `WFYardConsoleWindow`, and `colonies.yml` for
buildings and deposits.
