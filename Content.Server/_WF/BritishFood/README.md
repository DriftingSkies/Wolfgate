# BritishFood

British food, ported from Meridian Rift PR #242 (sprites by Mal). Twelve dishes to cook: hunter's chicken,
steak and ale pie, pork pie, chicken and mushroom pie, Cornish pasty, beans on toast, Welsh cawl, Eton mess, scones
with jam and cream, sticky toffee pudding, the deep fried Mars bar and the sausage, bean and cheese melt. Most bake in
the oven; the Mars bar is battered in the assembler and then fried in the deep fryer. Irn-Bru and dandelion and
burdock cans stock Robust Softdrinks.

Mrs. Luvvit's Pie & Mash (`WFVendingMachineLuvvits`) sells packaged versions, junkier than the cooked dishes and with
no Flavorol. One stands in the trade hall kiosk row on Caelestinus Central; its flatpack is sold by the premium
flatpack vendor and turns up in kitchen loot. Used in hand, a pack gives up its food and the empty container; the
food keeps everything left in the pack, fryer oil included. Hot packs come out piping hot (`WFPipingHotComponent`):
they steam for 10 to 30 seconds, and each bite that goes down before then burns the eater's head a little and puts
them off the next bite for 3 seconds. Food taken out of a hot pack, and slices of hot food, cool when it would have.
Eaters with pain numbness or a heat damage threshold of 400 K or more (lizards and hotter) don't notice it.

Each deep fried Mars bar holds 4 u of `WFSaturatedFat`. It reaches the blood after digestion and clears at 0.01 u/s
through the heart. Above 9 u (three bars within about four minutes) the `WFStrainHeart` effect takes heart health
through Wolfmed's organ damage, about 2 per unit over.

Entry points: `WFPipingHotSystem` (cooling, the scald, examine), `WFUnwrapFoodSystem` (taking food out of a pack),
`WFStrainHeartSystem` with the `WFStrainHeart` effect, and `WFPipingHotVisualsSystem` (the client's steam layer).
The prototypes are in `Resources/Prototypes/_WF/BritishFood`.

## Differences from Meridian Rift

- Masochists eat through the heat on Meridian; that part is left out.
- Ingredients follow SS14's: no swede, pavlova, caramel, cherry jelly or beer batter, so the recipes use what exists
  (raw pastry base for the hand pies, egg and sugar for the meringue, butter and sugar for the toffee, raisins for
  the dates, flour and egg for the batter). Tin-baked pies use a pie tin, as SS14 pies do.
- The cans have an opened sprite instead of a crushed one; SS14 cans aren't crushed.
- The vendor is mapped only on Caelestinus Central; elsewhere it comes as a flatpack instead of from the imported
  vendors crate or the summon beacon.
