# OutpostRaids

Raids on planet outposts, on unless `wf.outposts.raids` is turned off. Outposts can only be raided in a world's Active window:
from dusk (18:00 local) to dawn (06:00) and while a storm rages, unless a `wfRaidWindow` prototype gives the world
other hours or none (`WFRaidWindowSystem`). The outpost console's Overview and the time and weather console count
down to the window opening or closing.

`WFRaidDirectorSystem` decides the raids. Someone at home (on the outpost or inside its claim) builds its attention
(`WFRaidAttentionComponent`, saved with it) a point a minute, more for a bigger outpost. In the window, past its grace
(`wf.outposts.raid_grace`, from founding or loading), once a window and under the sector caps, the director spends it
on the biggest raid of the world's `wfRaidTable` (`raids.yml`) at or below the outpost's ladder step (`wfRaidTier`);
beating a raid moves it up to the table's next step. The raid walks in 40 to 60 tiles out from the claim, away from players, points of
interest and other grids, awake and loading the ground round it (`WFTerrainLoaderComponent`), to the claim edge and
then the outpost; it leaves at dawn or past its leash. The spot, the eight tiles round it and the edge point it heads
for are open ground, judged on unloaded ground by what the biome would lay there, so a rock that loads later never
walls a raid in or leaves it no path.

Step 2 opens a burrow (`WFRaidBurrowComponent` on a `FaunaBurrow`) at the walk-in spot: what it spawns joins the raid,
which isn't beaten until the burrow is broken or filled in with a shovel, and it collapses at dawn. Step 5 sets a raider
shuttle down beside the walk-in spot, outward from the claim and clear of other grids and the world's edge, held as an
outpost is (`WFRaidShuttleComponent`, `WFRaidPack.shuttle`); everything on it is raid loot, and it goes at dawn, or at
the next dawn if the raid is beaten before then. Step 6, the siege, sends its spawns again every `waveInterval`
(`waves`), and is beaten only once every wave is down.

Orbital defence (`WFOrbitalDefenceSystem`, `orbital.yml`, at the outpost outfitter) meets a step 5 shuttle on its way
down, and fires on raid shuttles only. With a powered sensor uplink (`WFSensorUplinkComponent`) or descent jammer
(`WFDescentJammerComponent`) on the outpost, the shuttle descends for three salvos, `wf.outposts.descent_salvo_interval`
apart, two more under a jammer, and the raid counts as on while it does. At each salvo, while an uplink tracks it,
every powered battery (`WFOrbitalBatteryComponent`: flak 15, orbital defence battery 40) takes its damage off the
shuttle's 100 hull. Shot down, the shuttle never lands and its first wave is lost, which beats a one-wave raid; else it
lands as before.

Steps 3 and 4 are humanoid raiders (`raiders.yml`, faction `WFOutpostRaiders`, hostile to people, pets and outpost
defences): scavengers, then a party with a breacher that rides in on a quad left where they walked in. A raider
(`WFRaidRaiderComponent`) keeps its own HTN, `WFRaidRaiderCompound`. At the claim edge a breacher gets the
`WFRaidBreach` key and runs `WFRaidBreachOperator` (`WFRaidBreachSystem`), the walk-up breach of spike F0.1: walk the
ground to just outside the edge tile nearest its goal, melee what blocks it, step in, and break inner walls the same
way. Its goal is the outpost's turrets, then its middle; the console is never attacked. What raiders carry is raid
loot (`WFRaidLootSystem`): cargo won't buy it, and an outpost save neither keeps it nor counts it in the appraisal or
sale.

On a world with a cavern, step 3 may instead send tunnellers (`WFRaidPack.tunnel`, `WFMobRaidTunneller`): tunnel raiders
who dig up from the cavern just outside the claim (`TryFindTunnelExit`), leaving a tunnel exit, and go straight on to the
outpost. Without a cavern they walk in like anyone else.

A raid on its way is held by `WFRaidSystem`. A powered early warning mast (`WFEarlyWarningComponent`, `warning.yml`:
120 s, or 240 s long-range) sets the outpost to ship code Red over its PA that long before the raid lands; without
one, everyone on the outpost gets a popup 15 s before. Defences (`defence.yml`) fire only on raiders and hostile
wildlife: the outpost turret (six an outpost, `wf.outposts.max_turrets`) and the perimeter mine (`WFFactionMineSystem`).
