# CorpseRadar

Five minutes after a player's character dies, its body shows on every ship's radar (mass scanner) as a small red
diamond, like a suit sensor that only reports a flatline. The wait keeps a quick revival or a fight still going on
from giving anything away. The marker is drawn on ships and in open space, within the usual 2048 m blip
range of the viewing console, and stays while the body is dead even after the player ghosts. Reviving the body removes
it; a gibbed or deleted body takes it along. NPC bodies get no marker.

Entry point: `WFCorpseRadarSystem`, which marks the body with `WFCorpseRadarComponent` on death and adds Mono's
`RadarBlipComponent` once the wait is over, so revival removes only the blip it added.
