# Vehicles

Driveable ground vehicles: RMC-14's vehicle framework with its vans, humvees, APCs and tanks, and Colonial Marines
Universe's vehicles (the PMC and TWE tanks, the AEV, the PMC APC, 17 civilian vehicles and the Bison remote cargo
carrier). A vehicle is a grid-locked body that a driver steers from a walkable interior on a private map; hardpoints
(wheels, treads, armour, turrets and guns) are fitted to it and take the damage. Vehicles are admin-spawned; there is no
supply console, lift or vendor.

The game has no xenonids, marines, skills, squads or RMC power, so everything that depends on them was stripped.
Wolfgate's own ride-on vehicles (`Content.Shared/Vehicle`: hoverbikes, janicarts, wheelchairs) are untouched; RMC's
base layer is renamed `RMCVehicle*` so the two cannot clash.

Players walk up to a vehicle's doorway and use it to go in, sit in a seat to drive or man a gun, steer with the move keys,
and use the horn, spotlight and overcharge button while driving. Leaving puts you on free floor you can walk to from the
hull, never behind a wall, a closed door or a deck edge; a vehicle walled in on every side keeps you inside. A prying tool
on the vehicle opens the hardpoint window to fit or remove parts; a welder repairs them. A gunner picks a hardpoint in the
seat's window or on the hotbar and aims with the cursor, and the turret turns to follow; rounds aimed at a point stop at
the first wall they meet, and the guns are fed from the interior's ammo loader, which loads boxes of rounds into a gun's
reserve. A vehicle smashes tables, lockers, fences, windows, unpowered doors and the like in its way (a heavier one goes
through more; a ship's reinforced windows and external airlocks only a tank breaks) and runs over people at speed
(`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/crushing.md`).
Driving off the edge of a deck where a z-level lies below drops the vehicle one level, hurting the frame, the wheels
and everyone inside; with nothing below, it stops at the edge (`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/falling.md`).

Admins spawn a vehicle by its prototype id. The roster is 50 vehicles (21 from RMC, 17 CMU civilian, 4 CMU military and 8
armed variants) and the Bison:

- RMC: the vans (`VehicleVan`, `VehicleBoxVan`, `VehiclePizzaVan`, `VehicleCLFVan`, the `VehicleSPPVan*` set), the humvees
  (`VehicleHumvee`, `VehicleHumveeMedical`, `VehicleHumveeTransport`, `VehicleHumveeARC`), the APCs (`VehicleAPC`,
  `VehicleAPCTracked`, `VehicleAPCMed`, `VehicleAPCCommand`, `VehicleSPPAPC`) and the tanks (`VehicleTank`, `VehicleSPPTank`,
  `VehicleSPPTankCommand`). The turrets of the tank and the APC start without guns; the `WFVehicle*Armed` twins
  (`WFVehicleTankArmed`, `WFVehicleAPCArmed`, `WFVehicleHumveeARCArmed` and the rest, `Docs/_WF/Vehicles/armed.md`)
  come with their weapons fitted, and the guns and their `VehicleAmmoBox*` crates can be spawned on their own.
- CMU military: `VehicleTankPMC`, `VehicleTankTWE`, `VehicleAev` (unarmed), `VehicleAPCPMC`, and the armed
  `WFVehicleTankPMCArmed` and `WFVehicleTankTWEArmed` (`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/cmu-military.md`).
- CMU civilian: the sixteen `AU14VehicleCiv*` vehicles and `VehicleCMBPoliceVan` (`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/cmu-civilian.md`).
- The Bison: `CMUCargoCarrier` and its handheld `CMUCargoCarrierController` (`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/bison.md`).

The client has the `rmc_vehicle_debug`, `rmc_vehicle_collision`, `rmc_vehicle_movement`, `rmc_vehicle_hardpoint` and
`rmc_vehicle_entries` overlays (admin only). Not here: flamers, the humvee ARC's deployable turret, riding on a tank's
roof, IFF, CMU's gameplay systems (failures, cook-off, maintenance, plows, wall smashing), and any way to get a vehicle but
admin spawn.

What it contains: the operator layer (`RMCVehicleSystem`, who drives) and the grid mover (`GridVehicleMoverSystem`, with
client prediction), which works in its grid's frame so it drives on decks that are turned, drifting or jumping to another
map, refuses to leave the floor unless a handler of `VehicleOpenTileAttemptEvent` allows the tile, and reads what it may
smash through `TryGetSmashable`; crushing rules for Wolfgate's own tables, lockers and the like (`VehicleSmashRuleSystem`,
`VehicleCrushingSystem`); falling through z-levels (`SharedVehicleFallSystem`, `VehicleFallSystem`); hardpoints, wheels,
integrity and repair (`HardpointSystem`); interiors on private maps with doorways, exit markers, locks and keys, driver and
weapon seats and the outside view (`VehicleInteriorSystem`); the horn, spotlight, overcharge, demolition and crash effect;
turrets and weapon seats with their gun hooks (`VehicleTurretSystem`, `VehicleWeaponsSystem`); hardpoint ammo, bullet
boxes, the ammo loader and port guns (`VehicleHardpointAmmoSystem`, `VehicleAmmoLoaderSystem`, `VehiclePortGunSystem`);
the Bison's remote control, cargo bay and self-destruct (`CMUCargoVehicleSystem`); and `VehicleSafetySystem` (server),
which keeps interiors from cleanup and edits, moves occupants out when a vehicle or its hull is deleted, counts them in a
ship sale and returns strays to the entry point. The new `CollisionGroup.Vehicle` bit (`1 << 30`) is on vehicle fixtures and
on the blockers mappers use to fence areas.

`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/design.md` has the decisions behind it, one note per area sits beside it, and
`Docs/_WF/Vehicles/port-manifest.md` records where each file came from and under which licence. The import, namespace,
map conversion and YAML translation scripts are in `Tools/_WF/Vehicles`; namespaces follow the folders (`design.md`).
