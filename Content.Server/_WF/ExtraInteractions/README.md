# ExtraInteractions

Small SS13-style hand interactions.

## Offering an item

Hand an item to someone without dropping it first.

- Press the offer key (J by default, G in the SS13 preset) or use the self verb to hold out the item in your active
  hand. A translucent copy of it floats in front of you; press the key again to lower it.
- Anyone in reach takes it into a free hand by clicking that copy, clicking you with an empty hand, or with the
  Take verb. The offer ends if the item leaves your hand or you go down.
- `WFItemOfferSystem` (shared, predicted) holds the logic; `WFItemOfferIconSystem` and `WFItemOfferOverlay` on the
  client draw the copy and turn clicks on it into a `WFTakeOfferedItemEvent`.

## Showing an item

Point at an item you carry, in a hand, inventory or storage slot, to show it off: a thought bubble with the item
pops up over you for a few seconds and chat gets "Name shows their crowbar." It shares the pointing cooldown.
Before this, pointing at a slot pointed at whatever lay in the world under the HUD.

- `WFShowItemUIController` (client) catches the point key over a slot and sends `WFShowItemRequestEvent`.
- `WFShowItemSystem` (server) checks the item is on the sender and sends `WFShowItemEvent` to everyone nearby.
- `WFShowItemOverlay` (client) draws the bubble, tg's `thought_bubble` sprite.
