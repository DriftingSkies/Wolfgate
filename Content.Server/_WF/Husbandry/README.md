# Husbandry

Keeping animals on an outpost. The build order is the F9 section of `Docs/_WF/Outposts/PLAN.md`.

Pens (`WFHusbandrySystem.GetPens`): an animal feeder (`WFOutpostAnimalFeeder`, `WFAnimalFeederComponent`) makes the floor
round it a pen when walls, fences, closed doors or other feeders close it off within 100 tiles; floor that runs off the
grid isn't a pen. Feeders in one pen add up, 6 animals each. Every 5 seconds the animals standing in an outpost's pens
that lay eggs, give milk or grow wool, or are livestock already, become livestock (`WFLivestockComponent`): passive, so
nothing hunts them and they hunt nothing, and aged from then on.

Records (`WFHusbandrySystem.Records.cs`): a save keeps no mobs, so as an outpost is saved its penned livestock is written
onto it (`WFLivestockRecordsComponent`: prototype, name, age, the time left until it next lays, gives milk or grows
wool, its feeder and its price) and taken off again once serialised. When the save lands the records are spawned in the
pen of the feeder each stood by, or in any pen with room, with their names, ages and cooldowns. Penned livestock counts
in the outpost's appraisal and fingerprint, through `WFOutpostAppraiseEvent` and `WFOutpostFingerprintEvent`; a staged
save counts its records instead. The cooldowns are set through small partials on `EggLayerSystem`, `UdderSystem` and
`WoolySystem`.

Catching (`WFHusbandrySystem.Lasso.cs`): a lasso (`WFLasso`, sold at the outpost outfitter) thrown over a living animal
nobody plays, within 4 m, takes a 3 second struggle a loop; an animal of 50 kg or more takes two. Caught, it is tied to
the thrower by a distance joint (`wf-lasso`, 3 m of rope or as far as it stood) drawn as a rope (`JointVisuals`), and a
planet's wildlife on a rope never retires. Using the lasso on it again, dropping the lasso, the animal going down or the
two ending up far apart lets go.

Taming (`WFHusbandrySystem.Taming.cs`): an animal with `WFTameableComponent` fed by hand (any food used on it, 2 seconds)
3 times inside 5 minutes is tamed and becomes livestock. Livestock is also kept from retiring as wildlife and from its
fauna leash, and runs `WFLivestockCompound`: it wanders like any animal until a herding whistle
(`WFHerdingWhistle`, at the outfitter) tells it otherwise. Blown, the whistle gives every animal of livestock within 12 m
its command: follow the whistler, stay where it stands, or go to the nearest pen with room within 40 m (alt-click picks
the command). The command is the HTN follow target, so a herded animal walks there and waits.

Natives (`Resources/Prototypes/_WF/Husbandry/livestock.yml`): each world has a tameable farm animal of its own in its
quieter fauna tables (ridgeback goat, meadow ox, dune fowl, musk goat, ash hen, spore cow, flesh hen) that doesn't
breathe the air, takes the world's heat or cold and gives a hide (`WFMaterialHide`) when butchered. While a world's
Active window is open (`WFRaidWindowSystem`), livestock that isn't in a pen is put in `WFLivestockPrey`, which every
faction that hunts by default hunts, and goes back to the passive faction when penned or once the window closes.
