# ExamineCard

Examining a character up close (shift-click within details range) turns the whole tooltip into an identity record
styled like a border-check ID card: a photo (their headshot if they have one, else their sprite), name, appearance,
species, the name and job on their ID with its job icon, a stamp, status chips, and a grid of worn and held items with
icons. The examine verb buttons sit in the card's footer. Nothing goes to chat, and the health and ID verbs need no
click: wounds show on the card as an icon strip per body part (sentence on hover), DETAILS opens the full wound rows
in a panel beside the card (and stays open for later examines), and any leftover text is printed as remarks. The
character's description shows cut off on the card, with its own DETAILS for the whole text beside it. The client
builds the card at once from what it already knows (`SharedExamineCardSystem.BuildCard`, predicted), so nothing plain
flashes up; the server's card, with chips and wounds, replaces it when it arrives.

The card changes with what the examiner can make out. Within details range it is the full IDENTITY RECORD
(PERSONAL RECORD on yourself). Beyond it, DISTANT OBSERVATION shows the face, build, held items and the coarse wound
look, but no ID or clothes. A refused examine (out of sight) gives a blank NO VISUAL card. The header band turns
rust for critical and dark red, tagged DECEASED, for the dead.

A character with an adult description (Anatomy tab) shows a heart beside DESCRIPTION, but only to an examiner who
may read their anatomy (`GenitalConsentSystem.CanExamine`: adult content on for both, adult characters, owner
present) and only while their face shows; the server never sends the text otherwise. The heart opens it beside the
card. Being examined tells the character in chat ("X looks you over", or "glances your way" from afar), at most once
per examiner every 20 seconds, never for ghosts; `wf.examine_card.notice` turns it off.

The stamp compares the face with the ID: ID MATCH, MISMATCH, UNVERIFIED when the face is covered (the name field
then reads "face covered"), or NO ID. Both strings are already visible on the card, so it reveals nothing new.

Chips: `ExamineChipRules` lists examine lines by Fluent id. The server renders each one for this character (as the
body and as its identity, held items left as wildcards) and sends the results as patterns; the client folds every
matching line out of the examine and health text, into a chip with the sentence on hover, or drops it when the card
already shows it (appearance, held items). The most specific pattern wins. Lines no rule covers stay as text under
the card. To chip a new line, add its id to `ExamineChipRules` and a `wf-examine-chip-*` label.

Entry points: the server `ExamineCardSystem` builds the card (`BuildCard`) and sends it from White's
`ExaminableCharacterSystem`; the client `ExamineCardSystem` rebuilds the tooltip when the server's examine info
arrives (Headshot's `ExamineServerInfoShownEvent`), and `ExamineCardText` does the line folding. It ignores the
Show detailed examine text option, since players turned that off to stop the chat spam; admins can fall back to the
old lines with `wf.examine_card.enabled`.
