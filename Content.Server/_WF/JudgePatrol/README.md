# JudgePatrol

Standing patrols round a station. A `wfStationPatrol` prototype names a station, an encounter and a ship count;
`WFStationPatrolSystem` spawns that many single-ship encounters on the orbit set by the encounter's Circle order,
evenly spaced, once a round is running with `wf.encounters.enabled` on. It paces the circling ships so they keep
their spacing, and replaces a lost or ended one after `respawn` seconds, opposite the gap the others leave.

`WFStationPatrolCaelestinus` keeps two judge ships (`WFEncounterJudgePatrol`: Hornet, Lightning, Petra or Roswell,
a pilot, a gunner and two guards) 2000 m out round Caelestinus Central. They fly as `WFJudges`, at war only with the
Reavers, the ADS and the corsairs: their NPC ships are fought on sight within 1500 m and driven 2500 m off. With
`npcTargetsOnly` their zones never take on a player ship, whatever its flag. Hostile drones
(`HostileDroneGrid`) within the patrol's `droneRange` (6000 m) of the station get an attack order from every ship,
which flies back to its orbit once the drone is down or gone; Caelestinus itself deletes drones inside 4000 m. Captains are never
warned or fired on, and a player vessel may land 40 stray ship hits in 30 s on one (`strayHits` on
`WFCrewProfileJudge`) before it is answered.

They mind known threats only (`knownThreatsOnly` on the encounter ship): a player ship with its IFF hidden or
unlabelled is not warned off or fired on as an unknown, as other patrols do, only answered once it fires on them.

The patrol encounters show in the admin encounter window and `wf_encounter list` like any other; ending one there
starts its respawn timer.
