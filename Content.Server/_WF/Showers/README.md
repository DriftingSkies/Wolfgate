# Showers

Working showers, ported from Meridian Rift (tgstation's `code/game/objects/structures/shower.dm`). Click one to turn it
on. Every second it sprays 5u from its 200u tank over everything on its tile. Each target gets a touch reaction from
every reagent in the tank, so plain water rinses Wolfmed chemical burns off, cools an overheating limb, puts out a
burning player and washes off cream pie. Anything else poured in sprays the same way.

While the tank is at least 70% water, a shower also restores stamina and washes fingerprints, fibres, DNA and
gunshot residue off its user and everything they wear or hold. It clears their bloody footprints and cleanable decals
on its tile too. The thermostat pulls body temperature towards warm (warms a hypothermic patient), freezing (cools
someone overheating, and hurts if left on) or scalding (heat damage every tick).

Tools: a screwdriver cycles the temperature (5 s), a multitool cycles the auto shut-off (until empty, 15 seconds,
forever), a crowbar disconnects the water recycler so it only sprays what you pour in, and a wrench takes it off to a
frame. The recycler refills 0.5u of water a second while it is off. Build it from the construction menu (2 steel, then
wrench the frame down).

Entry points: `WFShowerSystem` (toggle, tools, spray, `TrySetOn`), `WFShowerComponent`, `WFShowerVisualizerSystem`
(water tint and steam) and the `WFShower` / `WFShowerFrame` prototypes.
